## 1. A self-amplifying nerve-fibroblast circuit drives colorectal cancer progression — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-24
- **Authors**: Kobayashi H et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise a cholinergic nerve–CAF feedforward loop that promotes colorectal cancer (CRC) growth and EMT.
- **Method**: Chemogenetic mouse models, patient-derived CRC data; blockade of CHRM3 and NTN1 assessed in vivo.
- **Key findings**: CHRM3 activation induces CAF-derived NTN1, which amplifies intratumoral innervation; blocking CHRM3 or NTN1 improved mouse survival. High NTN1 expression associates with mesenchymal subtype and poor outcomes in human CRC.
- **Strengths**: Multi-model validation including chemogenetics, mouse survival endpoints, and human CRC cohort data.
- **Limitations**: Human data are observational; causality in patients not established.
- **Conclusion**: The CHRM3–NTN1 neuro-mesenchymal circuit is a therapeutically actionable driver of CRC progression.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.06.018 / PMID:42497870

## 2. The genomic landscape and treatment outcomes associated with CDKN2A/MTAP loss in patients with non-small cell lung cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Vokes NI et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise clinicogenomic features and treatment outcomes of CDKN2A/MTAP co-deletion (CMdel) in NSCLC.
- **Method**: Retrospective clinicogenomic analysis of 16,947 patients (primary) and 10,996 (validation) with paired DNA/RNA sequencing; time-to-next-treatment with risk-set adjustment.
- **Key findings**: CMdel occurred in 11.7% of NSCLC; associated with worse outcomes on ICI-chemo and chemotherapy regimens. >50% of CMdel tumours harboured actionable co-alterations, enriched in EGFR/ALK/RET.
- **Strengths**: Large dual-dataset validation; immune composition inferred by quanTIseq; actionable therapeutic context for PRMT5/MAT2A inhibitors.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective observational design; treatment allocation not randomised.
- **Conclusion**: CMdel defines a genomically distinct NSCLC subset that warrants biomarker-guided development of PRMT5/MAT2A inhibitors.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-0771 / PMID:42507545

## 3. Long-term Effect of the Medicaid Expansion Program on Cancer Mortality by U.S. Region, Gender and Race/Ethnicity — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Epidemiology
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Habib M et al.
- **Aims**: To assess whether Medicaid expansion was associated with reductions in cancer mortality overall and across subgroups.
- **Method**: Natural experiment using 2005–2019 state-level data; person-time weighted generalised synthetic control method comparing 27 expansion vs. 24 non-expansion states.
- **Key findings**: Expansion associated with overall cancer mortality reduction (MD −2.06; 95% CI −4.24 to 0.11). Significant reductions for women (MD −3.02) and non-Hispanic Whites (MD −2.27); Northeast states (MD −4.46).
- **Strengths**: Synthetic control method improves robustness to parallel-trends violations; disaggregated subgroup analyses across region, gender, and race/ethnicity.
- **Limitations**: State-level aggregate data preclude individual-level inference; minority subgroup estimates imprecise.
- **Conclusion**: Medicaid expansion had heterogeneous effects on cancer mortality, with clearest benefits for women and White individuals in Northeastern states.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1097/EDE.0000000000002030 / PMID:42504964

## 4. Romiplostim for chemotherapy induced thrombocytopenia in pediatric cancers — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-25
- **Authors**: Bansal S et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate prophylactic romiplostim for prevention of chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia (CIT) in children.
- **Method**: Investigator-initiated open-label RCT (n=93, age 1–14 years; AML, NHL, solid tumours); romiplostim 3 µg/kg vs. standard care; primary endpoint platelet recovery within 3 weeks.
- **Key findings**: Platelet recovery did not differ (91.3% vs. 88.1%; risk difference 3.2 pp; 95% CI −9.5 to 15.9). Clinically significant bleeding (≥WHO grade 2) was lower with romiplostim (0% vs. 14%; ARD −14 pp; P=0.01).
- **Strengths**: First RCT of romiplostim specifically in paediatric cancer patients; biological plausibility supported by IPF% increment.
- **Limitations**: Single-centre India setting; small sample; unblinded design limits bleeding endpoint objectivity.
- **Conclusion**: Prophylactic romiplostim did not hasten platelet recovery but reduced bleeding events in children receiving myelosuppressive chemotherapy.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03561-4 / PMID:42502080

## 5. Chinese expert consensus on precision testing and molecular diagnosis of pancreatic cancer (2025) — Score 3/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-24
- **Authors**: Cao D et al.
- **Aims**: To establish evidence-based guidelines for molecular testing in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).
- **Method**: Multidisciplinary CSCO Pancreatic Cancer Expert Committee consensus; synthesises evidence on biomarkers (KRAS, BRCA, MSI), liquid biopsy, and precision imaging.
- **Key findings**: Level 1 recommendation for genetic/molecular testing on tissue/cytology specimens to guide targeted therapy and immunotherapy; endorses WGS-informed molecular subtyping.
- **Strengths**: Authoritative national expert panel; integrates emerging precision-oncology biomarkers with clinical practice standards.
- **Limitations**: Consensus rather than primary evidence; may have limited generalisability outside Chinese healthcare context.
- **Conclusion**: A standardised molecular diagnostic framework for PDAC can optimise individualised treatment in clinical practice.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03522-x / PMID:42498744
