## 1. Timing of Initiation of Methadone Take-Home Dosing — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Intern Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-08
- **Authors**: Hossain MB et al.
- **Aims**: Assess how early vs. delayed take-home methadone initiation affects all-cause mortality and treatment discontinuation among OAT patients.
- **Method**: Population-based cohort (British Columbia, 2010–2022; n=9,788 incident + 31,658 prevalent new users) using target trial emulation; clone-censor-weight approach.
- **Key findings**: Take-home initiation within 5–12 weeks reduced 78-week mortality risk (ARD −0.98%, 95% CI −1.62 to −0.33) and discontinuation (ARD −5.29%) vs. no take-home. Any take-home outperformed no take-home.
- **Strengths**: Large population-based cohort; rigorous TTE design; multiple sensitivity analyses confirming robustness.
- **Limitations**: Observational; residual confounding possible; single Canadian province limits external generalisability.
- **Conclusion**: Early take-home methadone (5–24 weeks post-induction) maximises survival and retention benefits for OAT patients.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamainternmed.2026.3904 / PMID:42709433

## 2. ERBB2 Activating Mutations Promote Enhanced Internalization and Activity of Trastuzumab Deruxtecan in HER2-non-amplified Metastatic Breast Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Clin Cancer Res
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Mai N et al.
- **Aims**: Determine whether ERBB2 activating mutations predict T-DXd response in HER2-non-amplified metastatic breast cancer, including HER2 IHC 0 disease.
- **Method**: Real-world cohort (n=272) with Kaplan-Meier and multivariable Cox PH models; T-DXd internalization kinetics in isogenic ERBB2-mutant breast cell lines.
- **Key findings**: ERBB2 mutations independently associated with longer rwPFS (11 vs 6.2 months; HR 0.49, 95%CI 0.25–0.96). Mutant cells showed up to 1.96× faster T-DXd internalization and ~40× lower IC50.
- **Strengths**: Dual real-world and mechanistic validation; multivariable adjustment; covers HER2 IHC 0 subgroup.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective single-institution; small mutation group (n=20); potential selection bias in somatic sequencing availability.
- **Conclusion**: ERBB2 activating mutations independently predict T-DXd benefit in HER2-non-amplified breast cancer regardless of IHC score.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-0198 / PMID:42714867

## 3. O-mannosylation and Protein Maturation Checkpoints Represent Therapeutic Opportunities in BRAF Fusion Protein Oncogenesis — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Discov
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Borgenvik A et al.
- **Aims**: Define the role of BRAF fusion partners in oncogenicity and identify partner-specific therapeutic vulnerabilities in pediatric brain tumors.
- **Method**: Genome-wide CRISPR screens, POMT1/2 pharmacologic inhibition, and glycoproteomics in KIAA1549::BRAF cell lines and in vivo orthotopic models.
- **Key findings**: KIAA1549 confers specific POMT1/2 dependency; genetic or pharmacologic POMT1/2 inhibition reverses KIAA1549::BRAF-induced transformation independent of MAPK signalling.
- **Strengths**: Multi-modal validation (CRISPR, proteomics, in vivo); identifies MAPK-independent vulnerability; proof-of-concept for targeting fusion partners.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical proof-of-concept only; POMT1/2 inhibitors at early development stage; clinical translation unproven.
- **Conclusion**: Fusion-partner O-mannosylation dependency is a novel MAPK-independent therapeutic target in KIAA1549::BRAF pediatric brain tumors.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-25-1114 / PMID:42714308

## 4. Osimertinib After Definitive Chemoradiotherapy in Unresectable Stage III EGFR-Mutated NSCLC: Subsequent Treatments and PPO From the Phase III LAURA Study — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clin Cancer Res
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Özgüroğlu M et al.
- **Aims**: Report post-progression outcomes and OS interim analysis from the Phase III LAURA trial of osimertinib vs. placebo in unresectable stage III EGFR-mutated NSCLC.
- **Method**: Phase III RCT (2:1 osimertinib vs. placebo; n=216 randomised); pre-specified secondary endpoints: TFST, PFS2, TSST; second OS interim (31% maturity).
- **Key findings**: Osimertinib markedly improved TFST (HR 0.13, 95%CI 0.08–0.21), PFS2, TSST; OS trend favored osimertinib (HR 0.67, 95%CI 0.40–1.14) at 31% maturity.
- **Strengths**: Pre-specified secondary endpoints from a well-powered multinational Phase III RCT.
- **Limitations**: OS immature (31%); 90% placebo crossover to open-label osimertinib complicates OS interpretation.
- **Conclusion**: Osimertinib's clinical benefit extends beyond first progression, supporting its role in unresectable stage III EGFR-mutated NSCLC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-25-4523 / PMID:42714840

## 5. Glypican-3-Targeted Paired Radiotheranostics in Orthotopic Liver Cancer Models — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clin Cancer Res
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Lee W et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate GPC3-targeted paired PET imaging (89Zr-GC33) and alpha-particle therapy (225Ac-GC33) in orthotopic hepatocellular carcinoma models.
- **Method**: Preclinical biodistribution, QPID spectral autoradiography, bioluminescence imaging, and survival analysis in orthotopic HepG2 and Hep3B liver cancer models.
- **Key findings**: 225Ac-GC33 achieved excellent local control with complete pathologic responses; 89Zr-GC33 immunoPET response correlated with bioluminescence. Active in moderate-GPC3 Hep3B model.
- **Strengths**: Matched diagnostic-therapeutic pair with quantitative co-localization; two orthotopic models; multiple imaging modalities.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical only; single therapeutic dose tested; clinical translation and dosimetry in humans unknown.
- **Conclusion**: GPC3-targeted radiotheranostics demonstrate potent efficacy and companion imaging correlation in orthotopic liver cancer preclinical models.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1613 / PMID:42714869
