## 1. Somatic alterations in TP53, PIK3CA, and CCND1 reveal exposure-driven patterns in oral and oropharyngeal tumors — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: J Natl Cancer Inst
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-13
- **Authors**: Cortez Cardoso Penha R et al.
- **Aims**: Characterize how tobacco, alcohol, and HPV exposures shape somatic mutation profiles in oral/oropharyngeal cancers.
- **Method**: Retrospective genomic study of 1,086 tumors profiled with a 1,109-gene panel; TCGA cohort used for validation.
- **Key findings**: CCND1 amplifications highest in dual tobacco/alcohol users, replicated in TCGA and transcriptomically. APOBEC-driven PIK3CA mutations specific to HPV(+)OPC; TP53 enriched in HPV(−) smokers.
- **Strengths**: Large multi-center cohort, TCGA validation, transcriptomic replication of key findings.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective design; exposure self-reported; no clinical outcome follow-up.
- **Conclusion**: Distinct exposure-driven molecular patterns provide a framework for etiologic-based risk stratification in head and neck cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag267 / PMID:42593222

## 2. The selenium and vitamin E cancer prevention trial (SELECT): a secondary, post-intervention analysis on potential excess chronic disease incidence induced by selenium administration — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Am J Epidemiol
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-12
- **Authors**: Vinceti M et al.
- **Aims**: Assess long-term cancer and neurodegenerative disease risk from selenium supplementation in a large RCT.
- **Method**: Secondary analysis of SELECT RCT (34,887 men) linked to Medicare (through 2019) and National Death Index (through 2024); 341,697 person-years.
- **Key findings**: Selenium associated with higher risk of Hodgkin lymphoma, multiple myeloma, and possibly ALS. No excess lung or colorectal cancer risk observed.
- **Strengths**: Randomised design with multi-decade follow-up; linkage to Medicare and NDI strengthens ascertainment.
- **Limitations**: Few cases for rare outcomes; secondary analysis underpowered for low-frequency events.
- **Conclusion**: Organic selenium supplementation may carry long-term adverse risks for rare hematologic malignancies and neurodegeneration.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/aje/kwag189 / PMID:42581651

## 3. Association of high birth weight with risk of early-onset colorectal cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-11
- **Authors**: Mukherjee A et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate whether macrosomia or large-for-gestational-age birth weight increases early-onset colorectal cancer risk.
- **Method**: Nested case-control study at Kaiser Permanente Southern California; 471 eoCRC cases linked to California birth registry; conditional logistic regression with two-stage confounder adjustment.
- **Key findings**: Macrosomia OR 1.69 (95% CI: 0.97–2.97) for rectal cancer; LGA OR 1.81 (0.96–3.41); associations marginal and non-significant.
- **Strengths**: Record linkage with state birth registry; rigorous 10:1 matching; two-stage confounder adjustment.
- **Limitations**: Small linked sample (471 cases); marginal statistical significance; single health system limits generalisability.
- **Conclusion**: Intrauterine conditions may contribute to early-onset rectal cancer risk and warrant prospective evaluation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-25-1658 / PMID:42578973

## 4. First-line PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody plus chemotherapy in triple-negative breast cancer: a phase 2 trial — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-12
- **Authors**: Shao X et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate safety and efficacy of ivonescimab (PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody) plus chemotherapy as first-line treatment in advanced TNBC.
- **Method**: Open-label, single-arm, multicenter phase 2 trial; n=36; primary endpoints were safety and investigator-assessed ORR per RECIST v1.1.
- **Key findings**: ORR 80.0% (95% CI: 63.1–91.6%) including 2 complete responses; grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events in 58.3%; no treatment-related deaths.
- **Strengths**: Novel dual PD-1/VEGF blockade mechanism; multicenter design; 22.1 months median follow-up.
- **Limitations**: Small single-arm trial (n=36), no comparator; primarily Chinese centers.
- **Conclusion**: Ivonescimab plus chemotherapy shows encouraging first-line activity in TNBC and merits phase 3 investigation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04564-7 / PMID:42587052
