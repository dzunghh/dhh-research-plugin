## 1. Insights into early-onset colorectal cancer from a single-nucleus RNA-seq atlas of colorectal cancer across the age continuum — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Porter CBM et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise molecular differences between early-onset (<50y) and average-onset (≥50y) CRC using single-nucleus transcriptomics across the age continuum.
- **Method**: snRNA-seq atlas of 189 primary CRC tumours (54 EO-CRC, 135 AO-CRC), validated externally in 712 MSS CRC tumours by multispectral immunofluorescence and machine learning.
- **Key findings**: MSS EO-CRC showed increased stromal and decreased epithelial cell proportions versus AO-CRC. Left-sided EO-CRC had elevated epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition signalling via FN1/GDF/ACTIVIN pathways.
- **Strengths**: Age-continuous design with rigorous multi-cohort external validation; controlled for microsatellite status, sex, stage, and sidedness.
- **Limitations**: Cross-sectional design limits causal inference about age-related molecular changes in CRC.
- **Conclusion**: EO-CRC is biologically distinct with increased stromal crosstalk, providing a molecular-epidemiological atlas for the field.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04721-y / PMID:42957776

## 2. Benmelstobart With or Without Anlotinib After Chemoradiotherapy for Unresectable Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: The R-ALPS Randomized Clinical Trial — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Ji Y et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate benmelstobart ± anlotinib as consolidation therapy after chemoradiotherapy for unresectable stage III NSCLC.
- **Method**: Double-blind phase 3 RCT at 42 Chinese centres (N=553), two-stage design; primary endpoint IRC-assessed progression-free survival.
- **Key findings**: Benmelstobart + anlotinib improved median PFS vs placebo (15.1 vs 4.2 months, HR 0.49, p<0.001). Adding anlotinib to benmelstobart did not significantly improve PFS (HR 0.82, p=0.12).
- **Strengths**: Large multicenter RCT with double-blind design and independent review committee assessment.
- **Limitations**: Single-ethnicity (Chinese) population limits generalisability; grade 5 toxicity notably higher with combination (7.2% vs 1.4%).
- **Conclusion**: Benmelstobart consolidation significantly improves PFS in stage III NSCLC; anlotinib addition confers toxicity without additional PFS benefit.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3918 / PMID:42955021

## 3. KRAS-targeted therapy sensitivity depends on interferon signaling activation in pancreatic cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Zhang Y et al.
- **Aims**: To identify determinants of intrinsic resistance to KRAS-targeted therapy in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.
- **Method**: Integrated genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics profiling of 242 patient-derived organoids and matched tumour tissues; functional validation of IFN-β and KRAS inhibitor combinations.
- **Key findings**: Intrinsically resistant PDOs showed marked downregulation of innate immune/interferon signalling. IFN-β + KRAS inhibitors (MRTX1133, HRS-4642, RMC-6236) showed synergistic antitumour effects in resistant models.
- **Strengths**: Largest PDO multi-omics dataset for pancreatic KRAS resistance with matched tumour tissue validation across 242 specimens.
- **Limitations**: Organoid models may not fully recapitulate in vivo immune microenvironment relevant to clinical response.
- **Conclusion**: Interferon signalling activation is a key determinant of KRAS-inhibitor sensitivity; IFN-β combination therapy is a candidate strategy for resistant pancreatic cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.10.003 / PMID:42956743

## 4. A sex-specific platelet switch controls anti-tumor immunity in glioblastoma — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Sloan AR et al.
- **Aims**: To elucidate mechanisms underlying sex differences in glioblastoma outcomes via platelet-mediated immune regulation.
- **Method**: Pharmacologic and genetic PAR4 inhibition in murine GBM models; sex-stratified survival analysis, CD8T cell depletion, and human GBM patient platelet reactivity data.
- **Key findings**: PAR4 inhibition prolonged survival in female (but not male) murine GBM in an estrogen-dependent manner. Effect was mediated by enhanced CD8T cell infiltration and function in the tumour microenvironment.
- **Strengths**: Mechanistic clarity across multiple model systems linking platelet activation, sex hormones, and anti-tumour immunity; identifies PAR4 as a tractable target.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical murine results may not translate directly; no approved PAR4 inhibitor exists in oncology.
- **Conclusion**: Platelet PAR4 signalling is a sex-specific regulator of anti-tumour CD8T cell immunity in GBM, offering a new therapeutic avenue for female patients.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.10.004 / PMID:42956742

## 5. Intrinsic resistance to KRAS inhibition: Interfering with interferon — Score 2/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Miguel-López B et al.
- **Aims**: To summarise the multi-omics determinants of intrinsic KRAS-inhibitor resistance in pancreatic cancer, from the accompanying Zhang et al. study.
- **Method**: Preview/commentary synthesising multi-omics profiling and functional validation data from 242 PDAC patient-derived organoids.
- **Key findings**: Sustained MYC activity identified as the principal driver suppressing innate immune signalling in KRAS-inhibitor-resistant tumours.
- **Strengths**: Concise synthesis of mechanistic insights linking MYC, interferon suppression, and KRAS-therapy resistance in a high-visibility journal.
- **Limitations**: Preview article, not primary research; interpretive conclusions depend entirely on the Zhang et al. dataset.
- **Conclusion**: MYC-driven suppression of interferon signalling underpins intrinsic KRAS-inhibitor resistance, with implications for combination immunotherapy strategies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.10.001 / PMID:42956741
