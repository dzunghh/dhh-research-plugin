## 1. Sex Differences in Cancer Stage at Diagnosis of Nonreproductive Solid Organ Tumors in the United States, 2015 to 2022 — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-01
- **Authors**: Maclin BJ et al.
- **Aims**: Quantify sex differences in stage at diagnosis across 30 nonreproductive solid organ cancer sites using US population-level data.
- **Method**: Cross-sectional analysis of SEER 21 database (2015–2022, excl. 2020); polytomous logistic regression adjusting for age and diagnosis year; stratified by race/ethnicity and county-level income.
- **Key findings**: Males had significantly greater odds of regional/distant stage at diagnosis at 20 of 30 sites. Largest male-predominant aOR: tongue (aOR 2.34; 95% CI 2.16–2.53); largest female-predominant: bladder (aOR 0.69; 95% CI 0.66–0.72).
- **Strengths**: Large population-based SEER 21 registry spanning eight years and 30 cancer sites with stratified subgroup analyses.
- **Limitations**: Registry data cannot explain causal mechanisms; 2020 excluded due to COVID disruptions; cross-sectional design.
- **Conclusion**: Males are systematically diagnosed at later stages than females across most cancer sites, pointing to actionable diagnostic disparity targets.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-25-1826 / PMID:42381457

## 2. Adjuvant Pembrolizumab plus Belzutifan for Renal-Cell Carcinoma — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-02
- **Authors**: Choueiri TK et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate whether adding the HIF-2α inhibitor belzutifan to adjuvant pembrolizumab improves disease-free survival in resected high-risk clear-cell RCC.
- **Method**: Phase 3 double-blind RCT (LITESPARK-022); 1:1 randomisation, pembrolizumab 400 mg q6w ± belzutifan 120 mg daily for ≤1 year; n=1841; primary endpoint disease-free survival.
- **Key findings**: Pembrolizumab–belzutifan significantly improved disease-free survival vs. monotherapy (HR 0.72; 95% CI 0.59–0.87; P<0.001); estimated 24-month DFS 80.7% vs. 73.7%. Grade ≥3 AEs: 52.1% vs. 30.2%.
- **Strengths**: Large preregistered Phase 3 double-blind RCT with robust sample size and clinically meaningful DFS endpoint in an underserved adjuvant setting.
- **Limitations**: Interim OS analysis immature (29% of events); higher toxicity burden with the combination warrants long-term follow-up.
- **Conclusion**: Adding belzutifan to adjuvant pembrolizumab significantly reduces disease recurrence or death risk in high-risk clear-cell RCC after nephrectomy.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2518245 / PMID:42384869
