## 1. Nivolumab-Ipilimumab for Localized Microsatellite Instability-High Gastric/Gastroesophageal Junction Adenocarcinoma: The Phase 2 NEONIPIGA Nonrandomized Clinical Trial — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-15
- **Authors**: Samaille T et al.
- **Aims**: Report long-term EFS and OS after neoadjuvant nivolumab + ipilimumab in localized dMMR/MSI-H gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma.
- **Method**: French multicenter single-arm phase 2 trial (n=32); 6 cycles nivolumab + 2 cycles ipilimumab pre-surgery, adjuvant nivolumab for 9 cycles; median follow-up 48.3 months.
- **Key findings**: 4-year OS was 84.1% (95% CI 65.8–93.0%); 4-year EFS was 83.5% (95% CI 64.8–92.8%); pCR achieved in 59% (17/29) of surgical specimens.
- **Strengths**: Long follow-up (4 years) with OS and EFS endpoints; multicenter French cohort with prospective design.
- **Limitations**: Single-arm, non-randomized; n=32 too small to detect OS benefit over surgery alone.
- **Conclusion**: Neoadjuvant dual checkpoint blockade yields durable survival in MSI-H gastric cancer, supporting randomized organ-preserving trials.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.4162 / PMID:42879140

## 2. Talazoparib plus enzalutamide in high-volume metastatic hormone-naive prostate cancer: a randomized phase 2 trial — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-14
- **Authors**: Zacchi F et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate PARP + AR-pathway dual inhibition in biomarker-unselected high-volume mHNPC.
- **Method**: Randomized phase 2 trial (n=54); enzalutamide lead-in then 1:2 randomization to enzalutamide alone vs. talazoparib + enzalutamide; primary endpoint PSA <0.2 ng/mL at 12 months.
- **Key findings**: PSA <0.2 rate 73% (95% CI 56–86%) in combination arm vs. 64.7% in control; grade ≥3 AEs in 68% vs. 35%; two fatal myeloid neoplasms in combination arm.
- **Strengths**: Randomized design with paired tumor biopsies and AR/HRR transcriptomics providing mechanistic insight.
- **Limitations**: Small phase 2 (n=54); single-centre enrichment; no benefit over enzalutamide alone despite meeting primary endpoint.
- **Conclusion**: Talazoparib + enzalutamide is active in unselected mHNPC but increased toxicity warrants biomarker-selected phase 3 investigation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04671-5 / PMID:42875559

## 3. Cabergoline Suppresses Postpartum Breast Cancer by Enhancing Mammary Gland Involution in Brca1-Mutant Mice and Is Associated with Reduced Risk in Younger Women — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Discov
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-15
- **Authors**: García-Sancha N et al.
- **Aims**: Investigate cabergoline (dopamine agonist) as a chemoprevention strategy for postpartum breast cancer in BRCA1-mutant background.
- **Method**: Preclinical mouse model (K14-Cre; Brca1F/F, Trp53F/F) plus two independent retrospective European cohort studies and meta-analysis.
- **Key findings**: Single post-lactation cabergoline dose reduced postpartum mammary tumor incidence in mice; meta-analysis of human cohorts showed ~69% reduction in postpartum breast cancer risk.
- **Strengths**: Translational design converging mechanistic preclinical data with independent human retrospective cohorts and meta-analysis.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective epidemiological component; confounding by indication not fully excluded; cohort sizes not reported in abstract.
- **Conclusion**: Cabergoline is a promising low-risk chemoprevention candidate for postpartum breast cancer, particularly in BRCA1 carriers.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-26-0187 / PMID:42878664

## 4. Machine Learning Identifies Community-Level Factors Associated with Lung Cancer Incidence — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-15
- **Authors**: Pucillo GD et al.
- **Aims**: Identify community-level environmental, social, and economic drivers of geographic variation in lung cancer incidence in California.
- **Method**: Ecological analysis of 542 Medical Service Study Areas (AAIR 2015–2019); SVM with autoencoder feature selection and SHAP explainability; OLS regression as comparator.
- **Key findings**: SVM (15 features) achieved cross-validation AUC 0.891 vs. 0.685 for smoking-only model; SHAP identified smoking, linguistic isolation, neighbourhood SES, unemployment, and housing burden as top contributors.
- **Strengths**: Novel SHAP-based explainability applied to cancer incidence mapping; publicly available data enabling replication; highlights equity gaps.
- **Limitations**: Ecological design precludes individual-level causal inference; lower AUC in Hispanic subpopulation (0.744) reflects data gaps.
- **Conclusion**: ML with community-level indicators substantially improves identification of high-risk regions beyond smoking alone, supporting targeted prevention.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-26-0551 / PMID:42878648

## 5. Personalized neoantigen-targeting cancer vaccines in ovarian cancer: a phase 1 trial — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-14
- **Authors**: Sellars MC et al.
- **Aims**: Assess feasibility, safety, and immunogenicity of personalized neoantigen-based vaccine (NeoVax) combined with anti-PD-1 in platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer.
- **Method**: Phase 1 trial (n=10); synthetic long peptides targeting patient-specific neoantigens + poly-ICLC + delayed pembrolizumab; cfDNA monitoring of antigen stability.
- **Key findings**: Vaccine feasibility 75% (9/12 patients) in recurrent cohort; no treatment discontinuations; >70% targets detectable by cfDNA despite 6.5-month chemotherapy interval.
- **Strengths**: First neoantigen vaccine trial in ovarian cancer; cfDNA-based antigen persistence monitoring; mechanistic immune-escape clonal analysis.
- **Limitations**: Phase 1 with n=10; no control arm; no efficacy endpoint; small heterogeneous population limits interpretation.
- **Conclusion**: Personalized neoantigen vaccines are feasible and safe in ovarian cancer with evidence of immunological activity, warranting efficacy trials.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04717-8 / PMID:42875558
