## 1. Tarlatamab-Durvalumab Maintenance for Extensive-Stage Small-Cell Lung Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-24
- **Authors**: Pérol M et al.
- **Aims**: Assess whether adding tarlatamab (DLL3×CD3 bispecific T-cell engager) to durvalumab improves outcomes as first-line maintenance in ES-SCLC.
- **Method**: Phase 3 international open-label RCT (DeLLphi-305); 563 patients randomised post-chemoimmunotherapy; co-primary endpoints OS and PFS.
- **Key findings**: Median OS 21.1 vs 12.7 months (HR 0.42; 95% CI 0.32–0.54; P<0.001); median PFS 7.4 vs 1.9 months (HR 0.34; 95% CI 0.28–0.42; P<0.001).
- **Strengths**: Large international RCT with pre-specified interim analysis and dual survival endpoints; marks first meaningful OS benefit in ES-SCLC maintenance.
- **Limitations**: Open-label design; cytokine release syndrome (mainly grade 1–2) in ~50% of combination-arm patients.
- **Conclusion**: Tarlatamab-durvalumab maintenance approximately halves the risk of death versus durvalumab alone, representing a new standard-of-care candidate in ES-SCLC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2609482 / PMID:42929702

## 2. Sacituzumab Tirumotecan plus Pembrolizumab in PD-L1-Negative Advanced NSCLC — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-25
- **Authors**: Zhou F et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate the ADC sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab vs chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab as first-line therapy in PD-L1-negative advanced nonsquamous NSCLC.
- **Method**: Phase 3 open-label RCT (OptiTROP-Lung06); 455 patients; primary endpoint PFS by blinded independent central review.
- **Key findings**: Median PFS 15.0 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.49; 95% CI 0.36–0.65; P<0.001); OS HR 0.58 (immature data).
- **Strengths**: Addresses a major unmet need in PD-L1-negative NSCLC; nearly tripling of PFS is clinically meaningful.
- **Limitations**: Interim OS data immature; open-label; single-country enrolment (China).
- **Conclusion**: Sacituzumab tirumotecan + pembrolizumab substantially prolongs PFS over chemo-IO in PD-L1-negative advanced NSCLC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2611280 / PMID:42929694

## 3. Osimertinib-Savolitinib in Advanced NSCLC after Progression during EGFR-TKI Therapy — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-25
- **Authors**: Lu S et al.
- **Aims**: Compare osimertinib plus savolitinib (MET-TKI) versus platinum-pemetrexed in EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC with MET overexpression or amplification after osimertinib progression.
- **Method**: Phase 3 international open-label RCT (SAFFRON); 338 patients selected by MET IHC3+/≥10-copy amplification; primary endpoint PFS; secondary endpoint OS (final analysis).
- **Key findings**: PFS HR 0.55 (P<0.001); OS HR 0.68 (P=0.01); 12-month OS 70% vs 53%.
- **Strengths**: Precision-selected population by dual MET criteria; delivered both PFS and final OS benefit in a targeted resistance setting.
- **Limitations**: Restricted to MET-high patients; peripheral edema in 49% with combination therapy.
- **Conclusion**: Osimertinib-savolitinib improves both PFS and OS vs chemotherapy in EGFR-mutated NSCLC with MET-driven osimertinib resistance.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2611335 / PMID:42929693

## 4. Durvalumab, Tremelimumab, and Enfortumab Vedotin in Muscle-Invasive Bladder Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-24
- **Authors**: Powles T et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate perioperative durvalumab ± tremelimumab plus neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin versus surgery alone in cisplatin-ineligible muscle-invasive bladder cancer.
- **Method**: Phase 3 open-label 3-arm RCT (VOLGA); 695 patients; dual primary endpoint event-free survival for each durvalumab arm vs control.
- **Key findings**: Durvalumab-EV vs control: EFS HR 0.51 (P<0.001); 24-month OS 78.4% vs 69.4% (HR 0.58; P=0.003).
- **Strengths**: First phase 3 to demonstrate OS benefit with perioperative immunotherapy-ADC in MIBC for cisplatin-ineligible patients.
- **Limitations**: Open-label; high-grade AEs in 71.1% of durvalumab-EV patients.
- **Conclusion**: Perioperative durvalumab plus enfortumab vedotin significantly improves event-free and overall survival versus surgery alone in cisplatin-ineligible MIBC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2608323 / PMID:42929692

## 5. Anbenitamab plus HB1801 for HER2-Positive Advanced Breast Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-24
- **Authors**: Wang J et al.
- **Aims**: Compare anbenitamab (HER2 bispecific antibody) plus albumin-bound docetaxel (HB1801) versus THP as first-line therapy for HER2-positive advanced breast cancer.
- **Method**: Phase 3 multicenter open-label RCT (HEALER); 885 patients in China; primary endpoint PFS by blinded independent central review.
- **Key findings**: 24-month PFS 58.5% vs 34.4% (HR 0.46; P<0.001); ORR 87.6% vs 73.4%.
- **Strengths**: Large well-powered RCT; clinically substantial PFS improvement with a novel bispecific antibody in HER2+ breast cancer.
- **Limitations**: Conducted only in China, limiting generalizability; no OS data yet; THP is the sole comparator.
- **Conclusion**: Anbenitamab plus HB1801 demonstrates markedly superior PFS over THP, positioning it as a potential new first-line standard for HER2+ advanced breast cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2610345 / PMID:42929681

## Appraisal notes

This week's digest is dominated by practice-changing phase 3 RCTs from NEJM, reflecting a surge of precision oncology and immunotherapy-ADC combination trials across major tumour types — lung (SCLC, NSCLC), bladder, and breast cancer. A methodologically notable runner-up not selected (PMID:42909464, *Int J Epidemiol*) applied LASSO and Bayesian horseshoe regularised regression agnostically to pooled European/Canadian case-control data, identifying novel occupational lung cancer risk associations — a strong example of modern epi-methodology applied to cancer aetiology. Taken together, the week highlights both the clinical translation of targeted resistance-pathway combinations and growing use of machine-learning variable selection in large-scale cancer epidemiology.
