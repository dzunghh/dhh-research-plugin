## 1. Latent effector T cells mediate immunotherapy responses in the bone marrow microenvironment — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-10
- **Authors**: Kehl N et al.
- **Aims**: Characterise tumor-reactive T cells in bone marrow of MM and AML patients and build a clinical response classifier.
- **Method**: Single-cell multi-omics (TCR profiling, HLA immunopeptidomics, functional screening) across MM and AML patient cohorts with independent validation.
- **Key findings**: TFiT 15-gene signature identifies latent-competence effector T cells; TFiT score stratifies immunotherapy (not chemotherapy) response across independent MM and AML cohorts.
- **Strengths**: Multi-modal validation in independent clinical cohorts; integrates TCR, antigen landscape, and outcome data.
- **Limitations**: Bone marrow biopsy sampling may miss dynamic T cell trafficking; cohort sizes not reported in abstract.
- **Conclusion**: A latent anti-tumour T cell compartment in bone marrow malignancies can be quantified by TFiT and used to select immunotherapy responders.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.07.011 / PMID:42575099
