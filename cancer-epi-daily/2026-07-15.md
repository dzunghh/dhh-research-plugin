## 1. Birth certificate measures of maternal morbidity and their limits in abortion policy research — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: American Journal of Epidemiology
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-09
- **Authors**: Bell SO et al.
- **Aims**: Assess validity of birth certificate severe maternal morbidity (SMM) measures for state-level abortion policy evaluation.
- **Method**: Linked birth certificate and hospital discharge data from 12 US states (2018–2021); parallel trends testing across abortion-ban and non-ban states.
- **Key findings**: Birth certificates under-ascertained hysterectomy and blood transfusion; composite SMM appeared higher in ban states using birth certificates but similar or lower in hospitalization data. Ascertainment improved over time only in ban states, introducing differential data artifacts.
- **Strengths**: Multi-state dual-source design enables explicit measurement-error quantification and falsification testing.
- **Limitations**: Only 12 states; hospitalization data may carry its own measurement limitations.
- **Conclusion**: Reliance on birth certificate data in policy causal-inference studies can produce erroneous inferences from ascertainment trends, not true policy effects.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/aje/kwag159 / PMID:42425529

## 2. Establishing an efficacy hierarchy for breakthrough cancer pain management: a network meta-analysis of rapid-onset fentanyl formulations and morphine — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: BMC Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-08
- **Authors**: Tsao PH et al.
- **Aims**: Determine relative analgesic efficacy of all available rapid-onset opioid formulations for breakthrough cancer pain (BTP).
- **Method**: Frequentist random-effects network meta-analysis (NMA) of 17 RCTs (n=1,463) comparing 8 formulations at 10, 15, 30, and 60 min post-dose.
- **Key findings**: Intranasal fentanyl spray (INFS) ranked first at all time points (SMD 0.85 at 10 min; 95% CI 0.59–1.10). Oral morphine (MSIR) did not surpass placebo until 30 min (SMD 0.20; 95% CI 0.01–0.39).
- **Strengths**: Temporal analgesic profiling across all licensed formulations using P-scores provides an actionable clinical hierarchy.
- **Limitations**: Heterogeneity in BTP definitions and patient populations across the 17 included trials.
- **Conclusion**: INFS provides the fastest and most sustained analgesia for BTP; oral morphine's delayed onset should inform treatment selection.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1186/s12916-026-04993-8 / PMID:42420986

## 3. Spatial transcriptomic profiling of ovarian clear cell carcinoma reveals heterogeneity in OXPHOS and EMT gradients — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-08
- **Authors**: Truong Le T et al.
- **Aims**: Characterise spatial intratumoral heterogeneity in ovarian clear cell carcinoma (OCCC) across primary and metastatic sites.
- **Method**: Spatial transcriptomics of 21 primary-metastatic pairs (discovery) and 16 tumors in two validation cohorts; CosMx single-cell spatial profiling.
- **Key findings**: Inverse OXPHOS–inflammation relationship along the EMT gradient identified; LCN2 loss via SOX9 downregulation associated with partial EMT and worse outcomes. LCN2-high subclone linked to better prognosis in OCCC.
- **Strengths**: Discovery plus two independent validation cohorts with multi-platform spatial single-cell resolution.
- **Limitations**: OCCC is rare; findings may not generalise to other high-grade ovarian carcinomas.
- **Conclusion**: LCN2-SOX9 axis mediates EMT plasticity in OCCC and may serve as a prognostic marker.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-74593-w / PMID:42420312

## 4. Targeting ADGRG1 in cancer-associated adipocytes reprograms the immunosuppressive microenvironment and enhances immunotherapy responsiveness in triple-negative breast cancer models — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: BMC Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-09
- **Authors**: Zhou Z et al.
- **Aims**: Identify cancer-associated adipocyte (CAA) subpopulations driving immunosuppression in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) and validate therapeutic targets.
- **Method**: snRNA-seq of human adipose tissue; prognostic signature derived from TCGA-BRCA/METABRIC cohorts; in vitro co-culture and in vivo murine TNBC validation.
- **Key findings**: HAD7-CAA subpopulation enriched in aggressive TNBC; ADGRG1 knockdown combined with anti-PD-1 synergistically suppressed tumor growth and reversed immunosuppression in vivo.
- **Strengths**: Population-level cohort validation (TCGA/METABRIC) anchors mechanistic findings in human epidemiological data.
- **Limitations**: Mouse models may not fully replicate human TNBC microenvironment; ADGRG1 inhibitor specificity unestablished clinically.
- **Conclusion**: ADGRG1-CXCL8-AKT/mTOR axis in CAAs is a stromal therapeutic target that may overcome immunotherapy resistance in TNBC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1186/s12916-026-05049-7 / PMID:42426783
