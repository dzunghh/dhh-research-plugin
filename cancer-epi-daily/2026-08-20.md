## 1. Baseline circulating and tumor γδ T cells and early circulating CD8⁺PD1⁺ expansion predict response to Atezolizumab-bevacizumab in HCC — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-17
- **Authors**: Galy-Fauroux I et al.
- **Aims**: Identify circulating and tumour immune features predictive of oncological outcomes under Atezolizumab-Bevacizumab (AtezoBev) in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).
- **Method**: Prospective study (n=31); sequential PBMC immunophenotyping + whole-blood RNA-seq; validated in IMbrave150 RCT (n=209) and MSI-H CRC cohort (n=163).
- **Key findings**: High baseline γδ T cells associated with disease control (p=0.006) and longer PFS (p=0.02) exclusively in AtezoBev-treated patients. Early CD8⁺PD-1⁺ expansion correlated with longer PFS (p=0.03); CD8⁺TIGIT⁺ rise signalled resistance (p=0.04).
- **Strengths**: Multimodal immune profiling discovery validated in large independent RCT dataset (IMbrave150).
- **Limitations**: Small discovery cohort (n=31); retrospective use of RCT RNA-seq data.
- **Conclusion**: γδ T cells and early CD8⁺PD-1⁺ dynamics are predictive biomarkers of AtezoBev response in HCC and MSI-H CRC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1102 / PMID:42606323

## 2. Whole Genome HPV Liquid Biopsy for HPV-Associated Cancer Detection and Viral Physical State Classification — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-17
- **Authors**: Fisch AS et al.
- **Aims**: Assess diagnostic accuracy of HPV whole-genome sequencing liquid biopsy (HPV-DeepSeek) across HPV+ cancer types and test blood-based viral physical state classification (HPV-SIGNAL) for prognosis.
- **Method**: Multi-site prospective cohort of 235 HPV+ cancers across 9 anatomic sites; plasma whole-genome sequencing; computational classifier for episomal vs integrated viral states; three orthogonal tissue/blood validation approaches.
- **Key findings**: HPV-DeepSeek sensitivity and specificity both 99% across cancer types. Integrated viral states associated with worse PFS (HR 2.78, 95% CI 1.30–5.99, p=0.009) and OS (HR 2.95, 95% CI 1.01–8.61, p=0.048).
- **Strengths**: Multi-site validation across 9 anatomic sites with three independent orthogonal verification approaches.
- **Limitations**: Integrated-clonal group small (n=9); needs larger prospective validation.
- **Conclusion**: Blood-based HPV physical state classification provides high diagnostic accuracy and prognostic stratification at cancer diagnosis.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1467 / PMID:42606335

## 3. Rule-Based Identification of a Reliable Real-World Cancer Recurrence Endpoint — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiology, Biomarkers & Prevention
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-17
- **Authors**: Lavery JA et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate rule-based EHR algorithms to automatically detect cancer recurrence and its timing.
- **Method**: Algorithm comparison using a publicly available clinico-genomic database (breast, colorectal, NSCLC, pancreatic); varied anchor dates and evidence criteria; validated against manually curated institutional data.
- **Key findings**: Best algorithm (anchored 6 months post-diagnosis, ≥2 EHR reports) achieved sensitivity ≥0.95, PPV ≥0.81, NPV ≥0.84; estimated recurrence within median 1 month of reference.
- **Strengths**: Multi-cancer-type validation using a public dataset; clear, reproducible rule specification.
- **Limitations**: Cohort restricted to mortality data; cannot estimate recurrence risk in survivors.
- **Conclusion**: Scalable rule-based EHR algorithm enables reliable real-world cancer recurrence endpoints for regulatory and research use.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-26-0525 / PMID:42606327
