## 1. Molecular Features Underlying Discordance Between Clinical and Genomic Risk Stratification in Localized Prostate Cancer in Over 280,000 Patients — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-17
- **Authors**: Zelefsky MJ et al.
- **Aims**: To identify adverse molecular features (AMFs) associated with discordance between NCCN clinical and Decipher Genomic Classifier risk groups in prostate cancer.
- **Method**: Retrospective analysis of biopsy data from 287,145 patients; prognostic associations with distant metastasis evaluated in 15,109 high-risk patients.
- **Key findings**: 21% of NCCN low/intermediate-risk patients were genomically upgraded; TP53, PTEN inactivation, and AR activity drove discordance. High GC score + ≥1 AMF predicted 5-year DM probability of 17–25%.
- **Strengths**: Largest prostate cancer molecular-epidemiological dataset to date, integrating four distinct AMFs with validated endpoints.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective design; Decipher GC proprietary, limiting independent replication.
- **Conclusion**: Combined genomic and molecular feature profiling substantially improves risk stratification beyond NCCN clinical grouping alone.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-2348 / PMID:43043065

## 2. Partially Functional Hypomorphic Missense Variants in BRCA2 Are Reduced Penetrance Pathogenic Variants — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-17
- **Authors**: Huang H et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise hypomorphic missense variants in BRCA2 as a clinically actionable class of reduced-penetrance pathogenic variants (RPPVs).
- **Method**: Functional assays (HR repair, RAD51 foci, PARPi sensitivity) in 70 candidate variants; integrated with population-level clinical data and ClinGen classification framework.
- **Key findings**: 34 "strong" hypomorphs conferred moderate breast cancer risk and later diagnosis ages than full PVs; 32% of tumours with hypomorphs + LOH showed HRD vs 83% with full PVs.
- **Strengths**: Integrates functional, clinical, and variant curation evidence from multiple laboratory and clinical datasets.
- **Limitations**: HRD analysis was limited to tumours with available LOH data; clinical risk estimates require validation in prospective cohorts.
- **Conclusion**: Hypomorphic BRCA2 variants form a distinct category warranting tailored surveillance and eligibility for PARPi strategies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-79433-5 / PMID:43045764

## 3. CCNE1 Amplification Confers Poor Outcomes and Therapeutic Resistance in Breast Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-17
- **Authors**: Marra A et al.
- **Aims**: To define the clinicogenomic characteristics and prognostic impact of CCNE1 amplification across breast cancer subtypes.
- **Method**: Retrospective targeted sequencing analysis of 6,318 breast cancers from the MSK Breast Translational database using FACETS for allele-specific copy number.
- **Key findings**: CCNE1 amplification (4% overall; 8.7% in TNBC) associated with high chromosomal instability; significantly shorter PFS on first-line THP in HER2+ (8.4 vs 18.0 months, HR 2.33).
- **Strengths**: Large, well-annotated real-world dataset with subtype-specific survival analyses and genomic correlates.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective; PFS data incomplete for HR+/HER2- and TNBC subgroups; single-institution.
- **Conclusion**: CCNE1 amplification marks a genomically unstable, therapeutically resistant breast cancer subset supporting its use as a biomarker for novel therapies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-2631 / PMID:43042996

## 4. Molecular Residual Disease After Definitive Chemoradiotherapy in Locally Advanced Head and Neck Cancer: Clinical Value and Optimal Timing for ctDNA Assessment — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-17
- **Authors**: Bruixola G et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate plasma-only methylation-based ctDNA for molecular residual disease detection after chemoradiotherapy in LA-HNSCC.
- **Method**: Prospective cohort of 74 LA-HNSCC patients; ctDNA assessed at baseline, week 8, and week 12 post-CRT; compared with 12-week PET/CT.
- **Key findings**: Week-12 ctDNA specificity 100%, PPV 100% for recurrence; detectable ctDNA associated with 6.4× shorter PFS (HR 6.44, 95% CI 2.73–16.74).
- **Strengths**: Prospective design; first systematic assessment of optimal ctDNA timing using plasma-only methylation assay in HNSCC.
- **Limitations**: Small sample (n=74), single centre; negative predictive value only 76%.
- **Conclusion**: Plasma-only methylation ctDNA at week 12 is highly specific for post-CRT recurrence and complements PET/CT in prognostic stratification.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1545 / PMID:43043002

## 5. Longitudinal Detection of Circulating Tumor DNA in Children with Pleuropulmonary Blastoma: A Report from the International PPB/DICER1 Registry — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-17
- **Authors**: Schultz KAP et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate ctDNA utility for disease monitoring in pleuropulmonary blastoma (PPB) using personalised DICER1 hotspot assays.
- **Method**: Registry-based longitudinal study; 482 plasma samples from 73 PPB patients; droplet digital PCR targeting tumour-specific RNase IIIb hotspot variants.
- **Key findings**: 85% baseline detection in pre-operative type II/III PPB; ctDNA undetectable after resection; ctDNA preceded clinical progression by median 5.3 weeks.
- **Strengths**: Personalised liquid biopsy approach; large sample set for a rare paediatric tumour; multinational registry.
- **Limitations**: Type I PPB largely uninformative (ctDNA only detectable in somatic mosaicism); small per-patient sample numbers.
- **Conclusion**: DICER1-targeted ctDNA tracks disease burden and anticipates recurrence in type II/III PPB, supporting its role as a pharmacodynamic and surveillance biomarker.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1981 / PMID:43043066
