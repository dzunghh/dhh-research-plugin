## 1. Refining risk stratification of patients with early breast cancer eligible for adjuvant abemaciclib or ribociclib — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-14
- **Authors**: Arecco L et al.
- **Aims**: Compare baseline prognosis across monarchE and NATALEE eligibility-defined subgroups using pooled individual patient data.
- **Method**: Pooled IPD analysis from 3 pre-CDK4/6 inhibitor RCTs (N=4,795); Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression across eligibility subgroups.
- **Key findings**: monarchE high-risk features (≥5 cm or grade 3) in N1 patients yielded worse DFS (HR 1.56, 95% CI 1.27–1.92) and OS (HR 1.78, 95% CI 1.34–2.36); NATALEE-only N1 vs N0 did not discriminate 7-year outcomes.
- **Strengths**: Individual patient-level pooled data from three randomized trials with standardized eligibility mapping.
- **Limitations**: Pre-CDK4/6 era data; does not directly estimate treatment effect sizes under current criteria.
- **Conclusion**: monarchE high-risk features identify a clinically distinct N1 subgroup, with implications for future adjuvant CDK4/6 trial design.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag264 / PMID:42599204

## 2. The pan-tumor landscape, allelic status, and genomic complexity of SMARCA4 alterations — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-14
- **Authors**: Repetto M et al.
- **Aims**: Characterize SMARCA4 mutation classes across cancer types and link allelic status to genomic context and treatment outcomes.
- **Method**: Pan-cancer analysis of 68,920 tumor-normal paired samples via MSK-IMPACT NGS; OncoKB curation; clinical outcomes modeling in ICI and platinum cohorts.
- **Key findings**: SMARCA4 alterations most frequent in thymic tumors (10.7%) and NSCLC (7.1%); monoallelic class 2 alterations predicted BRM-degrader benefit, including one complete response in Phase I.
- **Strengths**: Largest pan-cancer SMARCA4 analysis with paired tumor-normal sequencing and prospective clinical outcomes linkage.
- **Limitations**: Single-institution cohort; BRM degrader outcomes from a small Phase I expansion cohort.
- **Conclusion**: SMARCA4 allelic status and mutation class define biologically and clinically distinct subsets relevant for emerging BRM-targeted therapy selection.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1069 / PMID:42599160

## 3. Peripheral Absolute Lymphocyte Count, Breast Cancer-Specific and Overall Mortality According to Race, Ethnicity, and Breast Cancer Subtypes — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiology, Biomarkers & Prevention
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-14
- **Authors**: Thompson CN et al.
- **Aims**: Investigate ALC associations with breast cancer-specific and overall mortality by subtype and race/ethnicity.
- **Method**: Registry-linked cohort (Oncoshare, n=11,669 stage I–III); logistic and Cox regression with race/ethnicity interaction testing.
- **Key findings**: Lower minimum post-diagnosis ALC associated with higher overall mortality (HR=0.96 per K/µL); effect strongest in TNBC (HR=0.73) and in NH Black and Hispanic women (interaction p<0.001).
- **Strengths**: Large diverse population-based cohort linked to California Cancer Registry and electronic health records.
- **Limitations**: Single-region registry; residual confounding by unmeasured comorbidities possible.
- **Conclusion**: ALC is a prognostic marker with differential racial/ethnic effects, highlighting immune-surveillance disparities in breast cancer outcomes.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-25-1950 / PMID:42599159

## 4. A Phase II Trial of Pembrolizumab plus Granulocyte-Macrophage Colony-Stimulating Factor in Advanced Biliary Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-14
- **Authors**: Kelley RK et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate efficacy and safety of pembrolizumab plus GM-CSF in advanced biliary cancers after prior chemotherapy.
- **Method**: Single-center Phase II trial (n=42); primary endpoint confirmed ORR; paired biopsies for tumor microenvironment analysis.
- **Key findings**: Confirmed ORR 12% (95% CI 4–26%); 26% had 6-month PFS ongoing; GM-CSF upregulated CD8+ T cells and antigen processing pathways in paired biopsies.
- **Strengths**: Translational paired-biopsy mechanistic data; includes PD-L1 biomarker subgroup analysis.
- **Limitations**: Single-center, small n=42; primary efficacy endpoint not met.
- **Conclusion**: GM-CSF elicited immunologic changes but combination did not meet pre-specified response threshold; paired biopsy data may guide future combination strategies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-25-3987 / PMID:42599167

## 5. EZH2 Inhibition and Myeloid Risk: A Feature of On-Target Biology — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-14
- **Authors**: Alumkal JJ et al.
- **Aims**: Contextualize tazemetostat's market withdrawal and argue for systematic target-mediated safety assessment in epigenetic therapy development.
- **Method**: Expert commentary integrating SYMPHONY-1 confirmatory trial data (5.7% secondary primary malignancy rate) with EZH2 biology in myeloid malignancies.
- **Key findings**: MDS/AML occurred in 5.7% of tazemetostat-treated vs 0% control patients; EZH2 loss-of-function biology in hematopoietic stem cells predicts target-mediated myeloid risk.
- **Strengths**: Mechanistically grounded argument linking EZH2 oncogene/suppressor duality to predictable clinical toxicity signals.
- **Limitations**: Commentary without new empirical data; causal inference limited to a single trial observation.
- **Conclusion**: EZH2 inhibitor development requires prospective monitoring of normal stem cell compartments; ongoing prostate cancer Phase III programs face particular scrutiny.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-2216 / PMID:42599162
