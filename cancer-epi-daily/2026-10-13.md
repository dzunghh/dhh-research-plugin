## 1. TOSYMA trial: digital breast tomosynthesis vs digital mammography — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Lancet Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-09
- **Authors**: Weigel S et al.
- **Aims**: Compare invasive interval cancer rates in women screened with DBT plus synthesised 2D vs standard digital mammography.
- **Method**: Multicentre open-label RCT (n=99,689 women aged 50–70) at 17 German sites; 24-month registry-linked follow-up; primary endpoint interval invasive cancer rate.
- **Key findings**: Interval rate 14.2 vs 18.6 per 10,000 (OR 0.76, 95% CI 0.55–1.06; p=0.11); 12-month rate significantly lower (OR 0.71, 95% CI 0.52–0.97).
- **Strengths**: Largest RCT of screening technology (~100k women); cancer registry linkage minimises ascertainment bias.
- **Limitations**: Primary endpoint not statistically significant; open-label design with potential reviewer bias.
- **Conclusion**: DBT plus s2D may reduce interval cancers by ~24% vs digital mammography, though primary endpoint missed significance.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/S1470-2045(26)00341-4 / PMID:42854706

## 2. New HPV microsimulation model for cervical cancer screening cost-effectiveness — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-09
- **Authors**: Campos NG et al.
- **Aims**: Develop a statistically identifiable HPV genotype-specific microsimulation model for evaluating cervical screening strategies.
- **Method**: Health decision model with novel statistical methods for intermittently-observed transition data; calibrated to São Paulo cohort for 13 carcinogenic HPV types.
- **Key findings**: Model closely matches age-specific HPV prevalence for all 13 types; projected median precancer age 30.0 years (95% CI 27.9–32.3).
- **Strengths**: Direct estimation of transition probabilities without calibration produces measurable SEs; generalisable to PAVE Consortium countries.
- **Limitations**: Currently validated in Brazilian population; comparative modelling vs established models pending.
- **Conclusion**: Novel statistically identifiable microsimulation enhances HPV screening cost-effectiveness analyses with quantifiable uncertainty.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag376 / PMID:42853653

## 3. Bavdegalutamide (PROTAC AR degrader) phase I/II in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-09
- **Authors**: Gao X et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate safety and efficacy of bavdegalutamide (ARV-110), a first-in-class PROTAC androgen receptor degrader, in mCRPC.
- **Method**: Phase I dose escalation (n=95) establishing RP2D 420 mg/day; phase II (n=144) in three AR molecular profile–defined subgroups.
- **Key findings**: PSA50 response 60% in AR T878/H875 mutation subgroup; median rPFS 11.1 months; no grade ≥4 treatment-related adverse events.
- **Strengths**: First PROTAC clinical trial; biomarker-stratified design; clear subgroup signal in defined mutation cohort.
- **Limitations**: Single-arm study; small mutation-defined subgroup sizes limit statistical power.
- **Conclusion**: Bavdegalutamide shows promising activity in AR T878/H875-mutant mCRPC with an acceptable safety profile.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-25-02995 / PMID:42854175

## 4. Multimodal deep learning for sentinel lymph node prediction in early breast cancer (SCAN-B) — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Zhang D et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate deep learning models combining gene expression and preoperative clinical data to predict sentinel lymph node macro-metastasis.
- **Method**: Retrospective cohort (n=6,836 SCAN-B); compared MLP, pathway-informed sparse NN, and Transformer models vs XGBoost; independent test set n=2,211.
- **Key findings**: Transformer + PreopClinic AUC 0.693 vs 0.596 (clinical alone; p<0.001); 27.2% SLNB avoidance at 92.1% sensitivity; TNBC AUC 0.734.
- **Strengths**: Large validation cohort; multiple DL architectures compared; biopsy-surgical specimen concordance demonstrated.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective single-cohort design; predominant use of surgical-specimen GEX limits preoperative applicability.
- **Conclusion**: Transformer-based multimodal GEX model substantially improves lymph node prediction and could guide axillary surgery decisions.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1134 / PMID:42848433

## 5. First-in-human trial of roginolisib (allosteric PI3Kδ inhibitor) in advanced/metastatic cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-09
- **Authors**: Di Giacomo AM et al.
- **Aims**: Assess safety, pharmacokinetics, and anti-tumour activity of roginolisib, a novel non-ATP-competitive PI3Kδ inhibitor, in solid and haematologic malignancies.
- **Method**: Phase I dose escalation (Part A, n=24) and metastatic uveal melanoma cohort (Part B, n=20); immunophenotyping and proteomics exploratory endpoints; NCT04328844.
- **Key findings**: Median OS 20.8 months in mUM; no dose-limiting toxicities; grade ≥3 TRAEs in 6.8%; decreased Treg abundance and increased activated CD8+ T cells.
- **Strengths**: Novel allosteric PI3Kδ locking mechanism; comprehensive immunophenotyping supports mechanism of action.
- **Limitations**: Small sample size; single-arm phase I/II; mUM focus limits generalisability.
- **Conclusion**: Roginolisib is safe with encouraging mOS in mUM; allosteric PI3Kδ inhibition with immune reprogramming merits further development.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-77358-7 / PMID:42855460

## Appraisal notes

This week's literature highlights a convergence of rigorous trial methodology and novel statistical approaches in cancer screening and prevention. The TOSYMA trial provides the strongest evidence yet on tomosynthesis breast screening with near-100,000 participants and registry-linked outcomes, while the HPV microsimulation paper from NCI demonstrates a methodological advance in identifiable state-transition modelling that should set a new standard for cervical cancer screening CEAs. The clinical trial entries reflect continued momentum in targeted therapy (PROTAC degraders in prostate cancer) and precision immuno-oncology, with growing use of multi-omic biomarker stratification to identify responder populations.
