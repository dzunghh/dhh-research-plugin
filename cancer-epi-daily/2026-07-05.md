## 1. Differences in overall survival for invasive epithelial ovarian cancer by race and ethnicity: results from the Ovarian Cancer Association Consortium — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-06-30
- **Authors**: Meagher NS et al.
- **Aims**: Quantify overall survival differences in epithelial ovarian cancer across racial/ethnic groups, including rarely studied Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander women.
- **Method**: Pooled analysis of 18 OCAC studies (n=14,552); Cox proportional hazards models adjusted for age, stage, year, histotype, BMI, smoking, and postmenopausal hormone use.
- **Key findings**: NHPI women had 58% higher mortality risk vs. NHW (HR=1.58, 95% CI 1.16–2.16); NHB women had 31% higher risk (HR=1.31, 95% CI 1.12–1.54). NHPI HGSC-specific HR=2.00.
- **Strengths**: Largest consortium study to disaggregate NHPI women in EOC; multi-institutional OCAC design with 14k+ participants.
- **Limitations**: NHPI subgroup small (n=98); no data on treatment quality or healthcare access differences by race.
- **Conclusion**: NHPI and NHB women face substantially poorer EOC survival, underscoring urgent need for equity-focused interventions.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03509-8 / PMID:42373846

## 2. Multilevel onsite training and mentorship model to accelerate early childhood cancer diagnosis in Northwest Ethiopia — Score 3/6

- **Journal**: PLoS Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-06-29
- **Authors**: Yimer MA et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate whether a multilevel training/mentorship model improves early recognition, referral, and diagnosis of childhood cancer in NW Ethiopia.
- **Method**: Quasi-experimental pre-post mixed-methods study across three care tiers; 47 clinicians trained + 1,020 health extension workers; 6-month mentorship; pre/post KAP surveys and chart reviews.
- **Key findings**: Median provider knowledge scores rose to ~91st percentile at both care levels; time from first contact to confirmed diagnosis fell 54.2%.
- **Strengths**: Mixed-methods design integrating quantitative KAP/chart review with qualitative CFIR analysis; relevant to LMIC childhood cancer early detection.
- **Limitations**: No control group; potential confounding from parallel public-health initiatives; limits causal inference.
- **Conclusion**: Multilevel training was associated with improved provider capacity and faster diagnosis; protocols are positioned for national scale-up.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1371/journal.pmed.1005132 / PMID:42371922
