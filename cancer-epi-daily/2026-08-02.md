## 1. Signatures of micropeptides encoded by lncRNAs in cancer progression and metastasis — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Zok S et al.
- **Aims**: Systematically identify lncRNA-encoded micropeptides and their pan-cancer association with disease progression across 17 cancer types.
- **Method**: Integrated computational and experimental analysis of TCGA expression data; structural modelling via AlphaFold for 3D fold prediction.
- **Key findings**: 478 lncRNA genes encode 1782 micropeptides (10–100 aa); 314 high-confidence MPs from 72 transitional lncRNAs are cancer type- and stage-specific.
- **Strengths**: Pan-cancer TCGA design with multi-method validation enhances generalisability of findings.
- **Limitations**: Most MPs predicted intrinsically disordered; experimental functional validation remains largely computational.
- **Conclusion**: lncRNA-derived MPs are a novel, underexplored class of cancer biomarkers linked to disease progression and metastasis.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03556-1 / PMID:42509464

## 2. Igniting antitumour immunity with cancer cell pyroptosis — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Reviews Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Liu X et al.
- **Aims**: Review gasdermin-mediated pyroptosis as a mechanism to trigger antitumour immunity and synergise with immunotherapy.
- **Method**: Systematic narrative review integrating GSDM biology, pyroptosis induction mechanisms, and preclinical/clinical immunotherapy data.
- **Key findings**: Partial cancer cell pyroptosis recruits immune infiltrates; T cell granzyme-mediated GSDM activation and small molecules can both trigger this cascade.
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive synthesis from leading structural and immunology labs; contextualises pyroptosis among other lytic cell death modalities.
- **Limitations**: Evidence base is predominantly preclinical; clinical translation of GSDM-targeting strategies remains early.
- **Conclusion**: Cancer cell pyroptosis is a tractable strategy to ignite antitumour immunity, with potential to synergise with checkpoint blockade.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41568-026-00959-3 / PMID:42509327

## 3. The Dendritic Cell-based Vaccine PROTEXI leverages Antiviral CD4 T cell Memory to boost anti-tumor immune responses in mice — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Kang JM et al.
- **Aims**: Develop a DC vaccine platform harnessing widespread pre-existing SARS-CoV-2 immunity to augment tumour-specific CD8 T cell responses.
- **Method**: Mouse melanoma and breast cancer models; DC vaccine co-presenting SARS-CoV-2 Spike CD4 epitopes alongside tumour-specific CD8 epitopes; humanised mouse validation.
- **Key findings**: PROTEXI significantly reduces tumour growth and improves survival; promotes T cell infiltration in immune-cold tumours and enhances checkpoint inhibitor responses.
- **Strengths**: Humanised mouse model validation and combination therapy data strengthen translational relevance.
- **Limitations**: Entirely preclinical; inter-individual variation in anti-SARS-CoV-2 CD4 memory may limit generalisability.
- **Conclusion**: Leveraging pre-existing antiviral CD4 memory via PROTEXI is a novel and potentially scalable DC vaccine strategy.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-74891-3 / PMID:42509244

## 4. FGFR4-associated APOBEC3 mutagenesis characterizes HER2-enriched subtype of human breast cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Jeong JY et al.
- **Aims**: Characterise APOBEC3-associated mutagenesis patterns in breast cancer subtypes and evaluate canine mammary tumour as a comparative model.
- **Method**: Genomic and transcriptomic profiling of human breast cancer with PAM50 subtyping; comparative analysis with canine mammary tumour specimens.
- **Key findings**: APOBEC3 mutagenesis strongly associates with the HER2-Enriched subtype; FGFR4 expression significantly correlates with APOBEC3A/B enzymatic activity.
- **Strengths**: Cross-species comparative design clarifies model limitations and reveals a novel FGFR4–APOBEC3 regulatory axis.
- **Limitations**: Canine mammary tumour proven unsuitable for A3 mutagenesis modelling; human sample sizes not reported in abstract.
- **Conclusion**: FGFR4–APOBEC3 activity is a candidate molecular classifier for the HER2-Enriched breast cancer subtype.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03517-8 / PMID:42509465

## 5. Cancer immunotherapy targeting murine myeloid cells requires endosomal pattern recognition — Score 3/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-27
- **Authors**: Pan Y et al.
- **Aims**: Investigate whether endosomal TLR signalling mediates the efficacy of anti-MARCO/anti-PD-L1 immunotherapy targeting tumour-associated macrophages.
- **Method**: Mouse tumour models with TLR9 and multi-TLR knockouts; mechanistic assessment of inflammasome component transcription in TAMs.
- **Key findings**: TLR9 absence renders TAMs unresponsive to anti-MARCO treatment while retaining immunosuppression; TLR9 regulates inflammasome component expression.
- **Strengths**: Rigorous genetic dissection of TLR-inflammasome axis provides mechanistic clarity on a known resistance pathway.
- **Limitations**: Mouse models only; relevance to human TAM heterogeneity requires prospective clinical validation.
- **Conclusion**: Endosomal TLR9 signalling is a prerequisite for TAM-targeting immunotherapy efficacy and a potential target to overcome resistance.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75543-2 / PMID:42509237
