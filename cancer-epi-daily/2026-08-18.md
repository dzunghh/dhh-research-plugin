## 1. Stereotactic Body Radiotherapy vs Moderately Hypofractionated IMRT for Localized Intermediate-Risk Prostate Cancer: A Randomized Clinical Trial — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: JAMA
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-13
- **Authors**: Ellis RJ et al.
- **Aims**: Determine whether SBRT is superior to MH-IMRT for patient-reported QoL and disease-free survival in intermediate-risk prostate cancer.
- **Method**: Phase 3 international open-label RCT (NRG-GU005); 136 centers; n=698 randomised to SBRT (36.25 Gy/5 fractions) vs MH-IMRT (70 Gy/28 or 60 Gy/20 fractions); EPIC-26 QoL and DFS co-primary endpoints.
- **Key findings**: SBRT significantly reduced bowel MCID (34.9% vs 43.8%, P=0.03) and grade 3+ GU events (0.6% vs 2.5%, P=0.04); 3-year DFS not superior (88.6% vs 92.1% for MH-IMRT).
- **Strengths**: Large multicenter phase 3 RCT with patient-reported primary outcomes and pre-specified DFS analysis.
- **Limitations**: Open-label design; median follow-up 3.2 years may be too short to capture long-term cancer-control differences.
- **Conclusion**: SBRT improves bowel QoL and reduces acute toxicity but does not outperform MH-IMRT for disease control at 3 years.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jama.2026.12627 / PMID:42593775

## 2. Tissue-Free vs Tumor-Informed ctDNA Assays for Molecular Residual Disease Detection in Early Triple Negative Breast Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-13
- **Authors**: Cunningham N et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate tissue-free ctDNA assay for MRD detection in TNBC surveillance and compare accuracy to tumor-informed approaches.
- **Method**: Prognostic analysis within multicenter phase 2 study (c-TRAK TN); n=159 TNBC patients; plasma every 3 months post-adjuvant therapy; tissue-free methylation-based assay vs dPCR and WES-powered tumor-informed assay.
- **Key findings**: Tissue-free detection strongly prognostic for recurrence (HR 27.2; 95% CI 13.7–54.2); median lead time 7.9 months vs 5.8 months for dPCR (HR 0.57, P=0.03).
- **Strengths**: Head-to-head comparison across two tumor-informed assay types; prospective multicenter plasma collection in a clinical trial.
- **Limitations**: Single-arm prognostic study; tissue-free assay detected ctDNA in only 34% of patients, potentially missing early residual disease.
- **Conclusion**: Tissue-free ctDNA surveillance is highly prognostic in early TNBC and supports clinical use when tumour tissue is unavailable.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.2833 / PMID:42593771

## 3. SYS6010, EGFR-Targeting Antibody-Drug Conjugate for Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Phase 1 Trial — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-13
- **Authors**: Li ZM et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate safety, recommended doses, and preliminary efficacy of SYS6010, a novel EGFR-targeting ADC, in advanced NSCLC.
- **Method**: Phase 1 first-in-human trial (ChiCTR2300072141); n=236 advanced NSCLC patients; dose-escalation with expansion at 4.2, 4.5, and 4.8 mg/kg across EGFR-mutant and wild-type subtypes.
- **Key findings**: ORR 34.7% in EGFR-mutant post-TKI+chemo cohort and 45.7% in post-TKI-only cohort; median OS 19.4 months in EGFR-mutant post-TKI+chemo group.
- **Strengths**: Largest phase 1 EGFR-ADC cohort to date; robust safety and pharmacokinetic data across multiple NSCLC subtypes.
- **Limitations**: Single-arm phase 1 design; predominantly Chinese patient population; phase 3 confirmation required.
- **Conclusion**: SYS6010 demonstrates promising antitumour activity and a manageable safety profile, supporting phase 2/3 development.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.07.012 / PMID:42594871

## 4. Metabolic Dysfunction After Initiation of Androgen Receptor Pathway Inhibitors in Prostate Cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-13
- **Authors**: Shaver AL et al.
- **Aims**: Characterise incidence and timing of metabolic syndrome during first year after initiating concurrent ADT and ARPI therapy.
- **Method**: Retrospective cohort study using national EHR data (Epic Cosmos); n=16,924 prostate cancer patients without prior MetS; Jan 2014–Sep 2025; cumulative incidence of MetS and components assessed by age group and ARPI type.
- **Key findings**: ~40% cumulative MetS incidence within first year; highest rate in ages 70–79 (51.7 events/1000 person-months); heterogeneity across ARPI types in exploratory analyses.
- **Strengths**: Large national real-world cohort enabling age- and drug-specific analyses not feasible in clinical trials.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective EHR design; MetS ascertainment depends on clinical documentation; residual confounding by indication likely.
- **Conclusion**: Early metabolic monitoring and multidisciplinary cardiometabolic care should be integrated into standard ADT+ARPI management.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.2790 / PMID:42593785
