## 1. Joint Effects of ALDH2 rs671 and Pathogenic Germline Variants in DNA Repair Genes on Risk of Aerodigestive and Gastrointestinal Cancers — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-27
- **Authors**: Tokunaga K et al.
- **Aims**: Characterize joint effects of ALDH2 rs671 and HRR/MMR pathogenic germline variants on multi-site cancer risk.
- **Method**: Case-control study (4,418 cases, 11,811 controls); targeted sequencing; Firth-penalized logistic regression stratified by alcohol intake.
- **Key findings**: MMR PGV + rs671 GA/AA conferred OR 95.6 for esophageal and OR 24.6 for colorectal cancer; cumulative CRC risk by age 80 was 87.7%.
- **Strengths**: Large multi-site sample with comprehensive germline sequencing and detailed alcohol-stratified interaction analysis.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective case-control design; Japanese population limits direct generalizability to other East Asian groups.
- **Conclusion**: Integrating ALDH2 rs671, DNA repair PGVs, and alcohol exposure substantially improves cancer risk stratification in East Asian populations.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-26-0629 / PMID:42944724

## 2. Online Updating Mixture Cure Models for Large-Scale Survival Data Streams — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Stat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-01 (Nov 2026)
- **Authors**: Jin L et al.
- **Aims**: Develop an online learning framework for mixture cure survival models without storing historical individual-level data.
- **Method**: Renewable penalized EM algorithm; simulation study plus SEER breast cancer data validation; tuning-free strategy.
- **Key findings**: Online estimators match batch-method accuracy while processing streaming data; validated on SEER breast cancer outcomes with scalable performance.
- **Strengths**: Computationally scalable, asymptotic properties established, simultaneous variable selection without full data retention.
- **Limitations**: Assumes stable data-generating distribution; performance under distributional drift not addressed.
- **Conclusion**: Online updating mixture cure models enable efficient large-scale cancer survival analysis without historical data storage.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1002/sim.70795 / PMID:42947606

## 3. Projected Obstetric Risks in an Aging Maternal Population in Taiwan: A Bayesian Age-Period-Cohort Analysis — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Int J Epidemiol
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-14
- **Authors**: Chuang HM et al.
- **Aims**: Project obstetric outcomes through 2050 in Taiwan as maternal age rises using Bayesian APC modeling.
- **Method**: Bayesian age-period-cohort analysis of linked NHI + birth certificate data, 2004–2021 (n = all singleton pregnancies).
- **Key findings**: Gestational hypertension projected at 26.4% and gestational diabetes at 60.9% by 2050; preterm birth projected to reach 11.2%.
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive 18-year national linked data accounting for age, period, and cohort effects with Bayesian credible intervals.
- **Limitations**: GDM estimates may be inflated by one-step screening adoption after 2010; Taiwan-specific findings limit generalizability.
- **Conclusion**: Demographic and generational transitions will substantially increase obstetric risks, requiring proactive prenatal care system adaptation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/ije/dyag230 / PMID:42945353

## 4. Hospital Diagnosed Chronic Diseases and Risk of Colorectal Cancer Recurrence in Stage I–III Patients — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-27
- **Authors**: Wheler J et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate whether prevalent chronic diseases at colorectal cancer diagnosis increase recurrence risk.
- **Method**: Nationwide Danish registry cohort, 39,239 stage I–III CRC patients post-curative resection 2002–2020; Cox regression with validated recurrence algorithm.
- **Key findings**: Overall comorbidity not associated with recurrence (5-yr HR 1.03, 0.98–1.08); mental health comorbidity showed HR 1.11 (1.02–1.21).
- **Strengths**: Nationwide sample with validated registry recurrence algorithm, comprehensive modified Charlson index including psychiatric disorders.
- **Limitations**: Hospital-based diagnoses may miss mild community-managed conditions; unmeasured treatment-adherence confounders.
- **Conclusion**: Poorer survival in CRC patients with chronic disease likely reflects treatment tolerance differences rather than higher recurrence rates.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-26-0829 / PMID:42944736

## 5. Calibrated Bayes Analysis of Cluster-Randomized Trials — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Stat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-01 (Nov 2026)
- **Authors**: Liu R et al.
- **Aims**: Propose calibrated Bayesian estimators for CRTs that achieve frequentist coverage even under working model misspecification.
- **Method**: Simulation study evaluating cluster-ATE and individual-ATE under misspecification and informative cluster sizes; Bayesian nonparametric working models explored.
- **Key findings**: Calibrated Bayesian estimators maintain 95% frequentist coverage under model misspecification; covariate adjustment further improves efficiency.
- **Strengths**: Addresses both estimand types with formal coverage guarantees; extends to nonparametric Bayesian frameworks.
- **Limitations**: Simulation-based evidence only; no real-data CRT application in cancer or public health.
- **Conclusion**: Calibrated Bayesian CRT methods offer robust, estimand-aligned inference regardless of model misspecification.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1002/sim.70797 / PMID:42934152
