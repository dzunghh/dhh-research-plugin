## 1. Context-Stratified Mendelian Randomization: Exploiting Regional Exposure Variation to Explore Causal Effect Heterogeneity and Nonlinearity — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Statistics in Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-01
- **Authors**: Burgess S et al.
- **Aims**: Propose a new MR stratification approach using exogenous geographic/contextual exposure variation to detect causal effect heterogeneity and nonlinearity.
- **Method**: Context-stratified MR with Cochran's Q and meta-regression; simulation validation; UK Biobank vitamin D–CAD applied example across 20 recruitment centres.
- **Key findings**: Method detects heterogeneity when present while maintaining nominal false-positive rates under homogeneity. No evidence for causal effect of vitamin D on CAD or heterogeneity across 20 centres.
- **Strengths**: Simulation-validated; avoids collider bias inherent in residual/doubly-ranked stratification; straightforward implementation.
- **Limitations**: Power depends critically on meaningful exogenous exposure variation between contexts; narrower exposure range than model-based stratification methods.
- **Conclusion**: Context-stratified MR is a credible, assumption-light alternative for exploring nonlinear and heterogeneous causal effects in large biobanks.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1002/sim.70727 / PMID:42680961

## 2. Prognostic value of circulating tumor DNA and copy-number alterations in patients receiving tandem [Ac]Ac-/[Lu]Lu-PSMA-617 therapy for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer: a prospective observational study — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: BMC Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-29
- **Authors**: Amghar M et al.
- **Aims**: Investigate ctDNA-derived tumor fraction and genome-wide copy-number alterations as non-invasive biomarkers of response and resistance to PSMA radioligand therapy in mCRPC.
- **Method**: Prospective observational; n=78 mCRPC patients; ultra-low-pass WGS from longitudinal plasma samples (n=172); Cox proportional hazards models including time-dependent TFx.
- **Key findings**: Time-dependent TFx associated with significantly increased progression risk (HR 4.9, 95% CI 1.2–20.1). High CNA burden associated with shorter median OS (8.3 vs 13.8 months).
- **Strengths**: Prospective design; longitudinal sampling; recurrent CNAs mapped to clinically actionable pathways (PTEN, RB1, BRCA2, ATM).
- **Limitations**: Single-arm observational; small sample (n=78); no independent validation cohort.
- **Conclusion**: ctDNA-derived TFx and CNA profiling are clinically informative biomarkers supporting real-time monitoring and stratification in PSMA-RLT-treated mCRPC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1186/s12885-026-16791-9 / PMID:42681619

## 3. Bladder cancer precision-cut tumor slices (PCTS) preserve functional immune microenvironment enabling ex vivo drug evaluation — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: BMC Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-29
- **Authors**: Richtmann S et al.
- **Aims**: Establish and validate a precision-cut tumor slice platform preserving native bladder cancer immune microenvironment for ex vivo immunotherapy evaluation.
- **Method**: Fresh patient bladder cancer specimens cultured up to 6 days; flow cytometry phenotyping; T-cell stimulation assays; nivolumab treatment in 9 patient samples; multiplex immunofluorescence.
- **Key findings**: PCTS maintained viability of cancer and immune cells for 6 days. Nivolumab showed dose-dependent immune activation in 2/9 (22%) samples, consistent with published clinical response rates.
- **Strengths**: Maintains native tumor-immune spatial architecture; functional immune readouts validated; correlates with clinical response rates.
- **Limitations**: Small n=9 for drug-response experiments; single-centre; no prospective clinical outcome correlation yet.
- **Conclusion**: This validated human-derived PCTS platform enables physiologically relevant ex vivo cancer immunotherapy evaluation and may reduce reliance on animal models.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1186/s12916-026-05173-4 / PMID:42681628

## 4. Integrative proteomics reveals MSH6 to modulate PARP inhibitor sensitivity in BRCA1/2-proficient ovarian cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: PLoS ONE
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-02
- **Authors**: Deng O et al.
- **Aims**: Identify molecular determinants of PARP inhibitor sensitivity in BRCA-proficient high-grade serous ovarian cancer (HGSOC) using chemical and ADP-ribosylation proteomics.
- **Method**: Chemical proteomics with rucaparib across BRCA-proficient HGSOC cell lines; CRISPR/siRNA MSH6 knockdown; ADP-ribosylation proteomics using clickable NAD⁺ analogs; mTOR signalling assays.
- **Key findings**: MSH6 enriched in PARP1 complex in sensitive cells; MSH6 knockdown decreased PARPi sensitivity. Co-treatment with mTORC1 inhibitors enhanced rucaparib effect in resistant cells.
- **Strengths**: Integrative multi-omic platform; identifies actionable MSH6-CHAF1A-mTOR axis; CRISPR validation.
- **Limitations**: Cell-line only; no patient-derived or in vivo validation; mechanistic findings require clinical translation.
- **Conclusion**: MSH6 modulates PARPi sensitivity via CHAF1A-mTOR signalling in BRCA-proficient HGSOC, offering a potential biomarker and combination therapy target.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0357365 / PMID:42685115

## 5. MR-based radiomics of mesorectal fat for improved prediction of perirectal lymph node metastasis and extramural venous invasion in locally advanced rectal cancer — Score 3/6

- **Journal**: PLoS ONE
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-02
- **Authors**: Swaengdee Y et al.
- **Aims**: Develop MRI-based radiomics models to predict residual EMVI and perirectal lymph node metastasis after neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy in locally advanced rectal cancer.
- **Method**: Retrospective; n=219 LARC patients; PyRadiomics feature extraction from post-nCRT T2-MRI; logistic regression, SVM, random forest; 5-fold cross-validation.
- **Key findings**: Combined tumor+mesorectal fat model achieved AUC 0.797 (EMVI) and 0.824 (PLN metastasis), outperforming single-region models.
- **Strengths**: Mesorectal fat features improve performance over tumour-only models; well-validated with repeated cross-validation.
- **Limitations**: Single-centre retrospective; no external validation; manual segmentation may limit reproducibility.
- **Conclusion**: Including mesorectal fat radiomics improves post-nCRT risk assessment in rectal cancer and may inform surgical planning.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0357268 / PMID:42685054
