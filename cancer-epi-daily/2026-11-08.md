## 1. Body Mass Index, Genetic Predisposition, and Obesity-Related Second Cancer Risk in Cancer Survivors — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Network Open
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-02
- **Authors**: Pfluger BA et al.
- **Aims**: To examine whether genetic predisposition to higher BMI is associated with obesity-related second primary cancer (SPC) risk in cancer survivors.
- **Method**: Prospective cohort study (Cancer Prevention Study II Nutrition Cohort, N=14,309 survivors); Cox models with polygenic score for BMI (PGS-BMI).
- **Key findings**: Highest PGS-BMI tertile had HR 1.26 (95% CI, 1.06–1.51) vs. lowest. High genetic predisposition + obesity combined yielded HR 1.82 (95% CI, 1.38–2.40).
- **Strengths**: Large prospective cohort with long follow-up, genomic data, and clear joint-exposure analyses.
- **Limitations**: Predominantly non-Hispanic White participants, limiting generalizability to diverse populations.
- **Conclusion**: Genetic susceptibility modestly elevates SPC risk, but obesity remains the dominant and modifiable driver for all survivors.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.42135 / PMID:42981092

## 2. Cost-Effectiveness of Remote Symptom Monitoring During Adjuvant Endocrine Therapy in Breast Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Network Open
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-02
- **Authors**: Hu X et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate cost-effectiveness of remote patient monitoring (RPM) for women on adjuvant endocrine therapy (AET) for early-stage breast cancer.
- **Method**: Economic evaluation embedded in the THRIVE RCT (N=304); probabilistic sensitivity analyses from societal perspective over 12 months.
- **Key findings**: App-only RPM reduced mean costs by ~$4,914 vs. enhanced usual care with similar QALYs (0.79 vs. 0.78); 68–78% probability of cost-effectiveness at $100,000/QALY.
- **Strengths**: RCT-based economic evaluation with full societal perspective and uncertainty quantification.
- **Limitations**: Single academic cancer-centre sample; complete-data sample limited to N=214.
- **Conclusion**: App-only RPM is a pragmatic, scalable, and likely cost-effective support strategy for AET adherence in breast cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.41402 / PMID:42970292

## 3. Hepatocyte IRE1α–XBP1s Axis Prevents Pro-Metastatic Niche Formation Through Metabolic Rewiring of Inflammatory Monocytes — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-03
- **Authors**: Zhang H et al.
- **Aims**: To determine how hepatocyte IRE1α–XBP1s signaling influences liver metastasis via non-cell-autonomous mechanisms.
- **Method**: Conditional knockout mouse models combined with single-cell metabolomics, glutamine-pathway blockade experiments, and patient survival correlation.
- **Key findings**: IRE1α/XBP1-null hepatocytes secrete glutamine via FoxO1–glutamine synthetase axis, recruiting inflammatory monocytes to promote metastasis; patient XBP1s expression positively correlated with survival.
- **Strengths**: Mechanistic pathway validated across multiple mouse models and corroborated by patient survival data.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical models; clinical translation of glutamine blockade requires further study.
- **Conclusion**: The hepatocyte–inflammatory monocyte glutamine axis is a potential actionable target to prevent liver metastasis.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-78812-2 / PMID:42977746

## 4. Reconstructing the Single-Cell Spatiotemporal Dynamics of Glioblastoma Invasion — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-02
- **Authors**: Mangukiya HB et al.
- **Aims**: To develop a quantitative framework for real-time tracking of glioblastoma invasion dynamics across patient-derived models.
- **Method**: GlioTrace—ex vivo confocal imaging + ML-based morphology classification + probabilistic Markov-chain state-transition modelling across six patient-derived xenograft brain-slice models.
- **Key findings**: Invasion plasticity is patient-specific and spatially organised; standard-of-care enriches a highly motile branching state; targeted therapies alter phenotype transition probabilities.
- **Strengths**: Novel multi-modal integrative framework with patient-derived material and quantitative probabilistic modelling.
- **Limitations**: Ex vivo brain-slice model may not recapitulate in vivo immune microenvironment dynamics.
- **Conclusion**: GlioTrace makes glioblastoma invasion a measurable, patient-specific dynamic process for comparing therapeutic responses.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-78908-9 / PMID:42971997
