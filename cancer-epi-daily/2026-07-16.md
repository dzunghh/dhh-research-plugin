## 1. Switching to camizestrant at ESR1 mutation emergence before disease progression during first-line treatment of hormone receptor-positive advanced breast cancer (SERENA-6) — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Lancet Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-13
- **Authors**: Turner NC et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate ctDNA-guided switching to camizestrant plus CDK4/6 inhibitor at ESR1 mutation emergence before radiological progression in HR+ advanced breast cancer.
- **Method**: Double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 RCT; 315 patients with ctDNA-detected ESR1 mutation randomised 1:1 across 264 sites in 23 countries.
- **Key findings**: Median PFS 16.8 vs 9.2 months (HR 0.45; p<0.0001); second PFS 25.7 vs 19.1 months (HR 0.63; p=0.0037).
- **Strengths**: First registrational study using prospective ctDNA surveillance to direct therapy switch before clinical progression; large multinational design.
- **Limitations**: Interim OS analysis non-significant; only ~17% of screened patients developed ESR1 mutations and were randomised.
- **Conclusion**: Switching to camizestrant at ctDNA-detected ESR1 mutation significantly extends both first- and second-line PFS.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/S1470-2045(26)00287-1 / PMID:42442380

## 2. Efficacy and safety of dabrafenib plus trametinib in adults with differentiated thyroid cancer: a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, phase 3 trial — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Lancet Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-13
- **Authors**: Gao M et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate dabrafenib plus trametinib versus placebo in previously treated radioactive iodine-refractory BRAF-positive differentiated thyroid cancer.
- **Method**: Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT; 153 patients from 42 sites in 11 countries; 2:1 randomisation stratified by prior VEGFR therapy.
- **Key findings**: PFS 12.8 vs 3.7 months (HR 0.38; p<0.0001); ORR 57% vs 4% (p<0.0001); interim OS non-significant.
- **Strengths**: First phase 3 evidence for BRAF-targeted combination in radioactive iodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer; multinational.
- **Limitations**: Small sample size (n=153); 86% Asian population limits generalizability; OS data immature.
- **Conclusion**: Dabrafenib plus trametinib is an efficacious second-line option for BRAF-positive radioactive iodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/S1470-2045(26)00133-6 / PMID:42442381

## 3. Virtual Sustained Tobacco Treatment for Patients With Cancer: A Randomized Controlled Trial (ECOG-ACRIN: EAQ171CD) — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: J Clin Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-13
- **Authors**: Park ER et al.
- **Aims**: To compare virtual sustained telehealth tobacco treatment plus NRT to NCI quitline referral in recently diagnosed cancer patients.
- **Method**: Multicenter RCT; 306 patients across 37 NCI Community Oncology Research Program sites; up to 11 telehealth counselling sessions plus 12-week free NRT vs enhanced usual care.
- **Key findings**: 6-month quit rate 28.4% vs 14.7%; VST participants nearly 4× more likely to use guideline-concordant cessation medication; incremental cost per quit $7,724.
- **Strengths**: Pragmatic multicenter nationwide RCT testing a scalable community telehealth model across diverse tumour types.
- **Limitations**: 86.3% White participants limits generalisability; $7,724 cost per quit may challenge broad adoption.
- **Conclusion**: Sustained virtual tobacco treatment nearly doubled 6-month quit rates versus quitline referral in a nationwide cancer population.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-25-02267 / PMID:42441935

## 4. Evaluating bias in target trial emulation for heart failure across statistical and deep learning methods — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nat Commun
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-13
- **Authors**: Fan Z et al.
- **Aims**: To assess whether advanced statistical and deep learning methods mitigate confounding-by-indication bias in target trial emulation (TTE) using RCT benchmarks.
- **Method**: Emulated trials of beta-blockers (positive control) and digoxin (negative control) in CPRD Aurum heart failure data; compared propensity score matching, IPTW, TMLE, and Transformer-based deep learning.
- **Key findings**: No method reproduced RCT benchmarks; all showed biased effect estimates; semi-synthetic simulation confirmed recovery only when all confounders observed.
- **Strengths**: Rigorous positive/negative control design with RCT benchmarks; comprehensive method comparison including state-of-the-art deep learning.
- **Limitations**: Heart failure setting; results may not generalise to cancer observational databases with different confounder structures.
- **Conclusion**: TTE cannot replace RCTs when confounding by indication is strong, even with advanced machine learning adjustment.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-74999-6 / PMID:42443168

## 5. Transcriptomic analysis of tissue-resident memory T cells of the fallopian tube reveals a precursor immune surveillance network for ovarian cancer prevention — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nat Commun
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-13
- **Authors**: Wang L et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise the immune landscape of the fallopian tube and its clonal relationship to high-grade serous ovarian cancer.
- **Method**: Single-cell RNA-seq and paired TCR-seq of tissue-resident memory-like T cells from matched fallopian tube, metastatic tumour, and blood.
- **Key findings**: 18.4% clonal overlap between fallopian tube and tumour-infiltrating T cells; FT TRMLs recognised autologous tumour antigens with IFN-γ responses and lower exhaustion than tumour counterparts.
- **Strengths**: Novel paired FT-tumour-blood profiling illuminating pre-neoplastic immune surveillance at the site of cancer origin.
- **Limitations**: Single-centre exploratory study; functional organoid assays limited in scale; no clinical prevention endpoint tested.
- **Conclusion**: The fallopian tube harbours a precursor immune surveillance network that may be leveraged for ovarian cancer immunotherapy and prevention strategies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-74599-4 / PMID:42443174
