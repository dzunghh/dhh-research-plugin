## 1. Lifestyle and genetic risk factors for postmenopausal breast cancer in the multiethnic cohort — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-15
- **Authors**: Zhao C et al.
- **Aims**: Examine whether a healthy lifestyle index score (HLIS) reduces postmenopausal breast cancer risk across ethnicities and polygenic risk strata.
- **Method**: Prospective cohort analysis (n=22,725) with multivariable Cox proportional hazards models; evaluated lifestyle×313-variant PRS interaction.
- **Key findings**: High HLIS associated with 33% lower breast cancer risk (HR=0.67; 95% CI: 0.57–0.79). No significant multiplicative interaction with PRS (P-interaction=0.48).
- **Strengths**: Large multiethnic sample (White, African American, Native Hawaiian, Japanese American, Latino) with validated genetic risk scores.
- **Limitations**: Self-reported lifestyle measures; ethnic-stratified estimates limited by subgroup sample sizes.
- **Conclusion**: Healthy lifestyle reduces postmenopausal breast cancer risk regardless of inherited genetic susceptibility across multiethnic populations.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag394 / PMID:42879705

## 3. Neoadjuvant stereotactic ablative radiotherapy before inferior vena cava thrombectomy for renal cell carcinoma: a phase 2 nonrandomized clinical trial — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-15
- **Authors**: Hannan R et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate feasibility and safety of neoadjuvant SABR targeting IVC tumor thrombus before nephrectomy/thrombectomy in RCC.
- **Method**: Single-centre prospective phase 2 nonrandomized trial (n=21 evaluable); SABR 40 Gy/5 fr or 36 Gy/3 fr; primary endpoint 1-year RFS vs. historical 55.7%.
- **Key findings**: 1-year RFS 71.4% (80% CI 56.6–82.0), meeting phase 2 criterion. No 90-day perioperative mortality; 4 grade 4 events, none radiation-attributed.
- **Strengths**: Long median follow-up (~9 years); novel biomarker data (Ki-67, ctDNA); first prospective evaluation of neoadjuvant SABR for this indication.
- **Limitations**: Single-centre, small n=21; nonrandomized design; phase 2 screening threshold interpretation requires caution.
- **Conclusion**: Neoadjuvant SABR before IVC thrombectomy appears feasible with acceptable safety; warrants randomized evaluation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.4172 / PMID:42879162
