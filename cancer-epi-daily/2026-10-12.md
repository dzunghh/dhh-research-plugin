## 1. Validated Clinical Calculator for Brain Metastasis Risk in Early Breast Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Raghavendra AS et al.
- **Aims**: Develop and externally validate a clinical calculator to predict brain metastasis risk at diagnosis of stage I-III breast cancer.
- **Method**: Retrospective cohort study; linear prediction model (BrMR) trained on n=40,282 (MD Anderson) and validated on n=1,644 (Institut Jules Bordet, Belgium); multiple imputation for missing values.
- **Key findings**: Brain metastasis occurred in 2,408/40,282 training patients; C-index 0.79 (training) and 0.76 (validation); AUC 0.81 at 4 years. IBC, HER2+, higher Ki-67, LVI, and higher T/N-stage predicted higher risk.
- **Strengths**: Large multicenter training cohort, independent international validation, practical online calculator tool.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective design; prospective validation needed before clinical surveillance implementation.
- **Conclusion**: BrMR accurately predicts brain metastasis risk and may support risk stratification and clinical trial enrichment.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag377 / PMID:42848614

## 2. Telomere Maintenance Mechanisms Improve Risk Stratification in Neuroblastoma — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Rosswog C et al.
- **Aims**: Test whether telomere maintenance mechanism (TMM) classification improves risk stratification in neuroblastoma versus standard of care.
- **Method**: Retrospective prognostic study; n=366 patients (test n=116, validation n=143); multivariable comparison of TMM vs. CNA-based and gene expression classifiers for EFS and OS.
- **Key findings**: TMM-based classification was the sole independent predictor in final multivariable models (EFS HR 6.4, 95%CI 3.9–10.6; OS HR 29.4, 95%CI 9.6–90.0; P<.001), outperforming both other molecular classifiers.
- **Strengths**: Independent test and validation cohorts; head-to-head comparison with two established molecular classifiers.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective; non-high-risk subgroup sample size relatively small.
- **Conclusion**: TMM-based classification provides superior neuroblastoma risk assessment and supports clinical implementation as a new stratification standard.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00742 / PMID:42848963

## 3. Spatial Omics Atlas Resolves Adrenergic and Mesenchymal Cell States in Neuroblastoma — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Patel AG et al.
- **Aims**: Characterize intra- and inter-patient cellular heterogeneity and clinically relevant cell states in neuroblastoma using spatial omics.
- **Method**: Analysis of 54 neuroblastoma tumors using sc/snRNA-seq, bulk RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, and spatial proteomics; new PDX-derived ADRN/MES signatures validated across multi-platform assays.
- **Key findings**: Established cell line-derived signatures failed to identify MES neuroblastoma cells in patient tumors; PDX-derived signatures enabled MES identification; high MES expression correlated with worse event-free and overall survival.
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive multi-platform atlas with transcriptomic, epigenomic, proteomic, ultrastructural, and functional validation.
- **Limitations**: Cross-sectional single-timepoint; PDX-derived signatures require prospective clinical validation.
- **Conclusion**: Spatial omics provides a framework for defining clinically relevant neuroblastoma cell states with prognostic implications.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.09.008 / PMID:42849441

## 4. Disitamab Vedotin Plus Tislelizumab in Unresectable ERBB2-Positive Bladder Cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Qie Y et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate efficacy and safety of disitamab vedotin (anti-ERBB2 ADC) plus tislelizumab (PD-1 inhibitor) in transurethrally unresectable ERBB2-positive very high-risk NMIBC.
- **Method**: Open-label, single-arm, phase 2 nonrandomized clinical trial; Simon 2-stage design; n=28 patients ineligible for/declining cystectomy; median follow-up 29.4 months.
- **Key findings**: CR of high-risk disease achieved in 19/26 efficacy-evaluable patients (73.1%; 95%CI 54.8–91.3%); 24-month sustained response 83.5%; grade 3–4 adverse events in 14.3%.
- **Strengths**: Addresses critical unmet need for cystectomy-ineligible patients; durable response with nearly 2.5-year follow-up.
- **Limitations**: Small sample (n=28), nonrandomized single-arm design; no comparator group.
- **Conclusion**: DV plus tislelizumab demonstrates high CR rate and durable disease control, supporting randomized trial development.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3909 / PMID:42848391
