## 1. Rb-driven transcription limits its tumour-suppressive effects in breast cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-12
- **Authors**: Watt AC et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise how CDK4/6 inhibition remodels Rb chromatin binding and transcription beyond cell cycle arrest in HR+ breast cancer.
- **Method**: Mechanistic study using ChIP-seq, patient-derived xenografts, and clinical HR+ breast cancer samples with CDK4/6 inhibitor treatment.
- **Key findings**: Pharmacologically activated Rb redistributes to enhancers and unexpectedly promotes ER target gene expression, including pro-proliferative programs. In ESR1-mutant endocrine-resistant tumours, this program persists and limits CDK4/6 inhibitor efficacy.
- **Strengths**: Multi-platform validation (cell lines, PDX, clinical samples) with mechanistic dissection of KDM5A–Rb interaction.
- **Limitations**: Predominantly preclinical; clinical validation in ESR1-mutant cohorts is limited.
- **Conclusion**: Rb functions as a dual transcriptional regulator that can counteract its own tumour-suppressor function via ER-programme activation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10886-w / PMID:42587147

## 2. Biomarkers of nivolumab benefit in resectable non-small cell lung cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-12
- **Authors**: Cascone T et al.
- **Aims**: To identify predictive biomarkers of perioperative nivolumab benefit in resectable NSCLC using the CheckMate 77T RCT.
- **Method**: Biomarker substudy (n=190 evaluable) of a phase 3 RCT; ctDNA clearance, MRD, genomic co-alterations, and machine-learning model applied.
- **Key findings**: Pre-surgical ctDNA clearance occurred in 66% of nivolumab patients vs 38% placebo; ML top predictors of prolonged EFS included ctDNA clearance, non-N2 stage, pCR, and squamous histology.
- **Strengths**: Prospective RCT design with multi-biomarker integration and ML-based predictor model; multicentre international cohort.
- **Limitations**: Only 41% of randomised patients had evaluable biomarkers; industry-sponsored with potential selection bias.
- **Conclusion**: ctDNA clearance and pCR are key predictors of EFS benefit with perioperative nivolumab in resectable NSCLC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10925-6 / PMID:42587156
