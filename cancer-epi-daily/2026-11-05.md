## 1. 5-Year Outcomes of Chemotherapy De-Escalation in HER2-Positive Early Breast Cancer (PHERGain Trial) — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Pérez-García JM et al.
- **Aims**: Report prespecified 5-year iDFS and exploratory ctDNA outcomes from the PET-guided chemotherapy de-escalation PHERGain trial.
- **Method**: Randomized phase 2 trial; FDG-PET-guided, pCR-adapted strategy to omit chemotherapy in HER2+ stages I–IIIA breast cancer.
- **Key findings**: 5-year iDFS 89.5% (95% CI 85.7–93.4) in group B; 92.4% among PET-responders achieving pCR without chemotherapy. ctDNA clearance correlated with higher pCR and better long-term outcomes.
- **Strengths**: Prespecified 5-year endpoint; ctDNA integrated prospectively alongside clinical endpoints.
- **Limitations**: PET-responder pCR subgroup outcomes are post-randomization and descriptive, not causal; not powered for comparative analysis.
- **Conclusion**: FDG-PET-guided pCR-adapted de-escalation remains clinically valid; ctDNA shows potential to refine treatment individualization.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04723-w / PMID:42957778

## 2. Methylphenidate for Hematologic Cancer-Related Fatigue: A Randomized Clinical Trial — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-30
- **Authors**: Frederiksen H et al.
- **Aims**: Determine whether methylphenidate reduces cancer-related fatigue in adult patients with hematologic cancer.
- **Method**: Double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-period crossover RCT; n=152 fatigued adults with hematologic cancer; 6-week treatment periods.
- **Key findings**: FACIT-F improved 8.9 points with methylphenidate vs 3.9 with placebo; between-group difference 5.0 (95% CI 2.8–7.2). More adverse events with methylphenidate (57% vs 40%).
- **Strengths**: Double-blind crossover design controls individual variation; uses validated fatigue outcome measure.
- **Limitations**: Excluded patients on chemotherapy or concomitant psychopharmacology; moderate adverse event rate may limit uptake.
- **Conclusion**: Methylphenidate significantly alleviated fatigue and can be considered as pharmacologic intervention in hematologic cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00578 / PMID:42962158

## 3. Genome-Wide Screen in E. coli Reveals Metabolic Adaptations Enhancing Bacterial Cancer Therapy Efficacy — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Chen N et al.
- **Aims**: Identify bacterial genes governing intratumoral fitness to engineer more effective bacterial cancer therapy chassis.
- **Method**: Genome-wide functional screen in E. coli; metabolic engineering of tumor-avid strain (Ec-TA); in vivo tumor colonization and anti-tumor efficacy assays.
- **Key findings**: Ec-TA showed increased tumor colonization via both intratumoral and intravenous delivery; Ec-TA displaced wild-type bacteria from pre-colonized tumors when administered systemically.
- **Strengths**: Unbiased genome-wide functional screen comprehensively identifies fitness-governing genes; demonstrates in vivo proof of concept.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical animal models only; clinical translation of engineered live bacteria involves major safety and regulatory hurdles.
- **Conclusion**: Adapting bacteria metabolically to the tumor microenvironment produces safer, more effective chassis for next-generation bacterial cancer therapies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s43018-026-01263-7 / PMID:42957894

## 4. Chemoresistant Cell Population Profiling Improves Risk Stratification and Therapy Prediction in Pediatric AML — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-02
- **Authors**: NajafPanah MJ et al.
- **Aims**: Improve risk prediction in pediatric AML by integrating chemoresistant cell population detection with standard biomarkers.
- **Method**: Single-cell transcriptomics of paired diagnosis-relapse pAML samples; combined with cytogenetic biomarkers and measurable residual disease (MRD) after induction.
- **Key findings**: Combined approach identifies a 20% patient subgroup (non-transplant) with 5-year EFS <40%, accounting for ~50% of all pAML deaths.
- **Strengths**: Single-cell resolution reveals specific chemoresistant populations; multi-modal biomarker integration adds actionable therapeutic targets.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective paired sample design; requires specialized single-cell transcriptomic infrastructure limiting broad clinical applicability.
- **Conclusion**: Integrating chemoresistant cell profiles with cytogenetics and MRD substantially improves risk prediction and guides precision oncology in pAML.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-78283-5 / PMID:42957737

## 5. Caregivers and Legal Barriers: Navigating Care of a Family Member Diagnosed with Cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-29
- **Authors**: Chen KY et al.
- **Aims**: Characterize the legal and administrative needs of cancer patient caregivers navigating insurance, employment, and benefits systems.
- **Method**: Cross-sectional analysis of 1,091 caregiver-initiated consultations from a national non-profit legal/financial navigation program, 2021–2024.
- **Key findings**: 1,889 legal concerns documented; health insurance (32.1%) and finances (24.0%) most common. >51% reported multiple concurrent barriers; Southern US residence associated with higher odds (OR 1.60).
- **Strengths**: Large national program dataset; systematic documentation across diverse insurance types and geographic regions.
- **Limitations**: Single-program, self-selected sample; likely under-represents caregivers without access to legal navigation services.
- **Conclusion**: Caregiver-focused legal navigation screening and education should be integrated into standard supportive oncology care.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag398 / PMID:42958655
