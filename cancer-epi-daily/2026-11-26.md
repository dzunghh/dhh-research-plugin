## 1. Alectinib as Neoadjuvant Treatment in Potentially Resectable Stage III ALK-Positive NSCLC: ALNEO Phase II Trial — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: J Clin Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-20
- **Authors**: Leonetti A et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate activity and safety of perioperative alectinib in treatment-naïve resectable stage III ALK+ NSCLC.
- **Method**: Open-label single-arm multicenter phase II; 8-week neoadjuvant alectinib then surgery then adjuvant alectinib ≤96 weeks; primary endpoint major pathologic response (MPR) by blinded central review.
- **Key findings**: MPR achieved in 42% (95% CI 26–61); pCR 12%; ORR 67%; median EFS/DFS/OS not reached at 26.8-month follow-up.
- **Strengths**: First prospective perioperative ALK inhibitor trial in stage III; blinded independent central pathologic review.
- **Limitations**: Single-arm, no control; small n=33; follow-up too short for mature survival endpoints.
- **Conclusion**: Perioperative alectinib is feasible and active in resectable stage III ALK+ NSCLC, meeting the primary MPR endpoint.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00854 / PMID:43063368

## 2. Effect of a Multidisciplinary Intervention on Fatigue in Lymphoma Survivors With Persistent Fatigue: A Randomized Controlled Trial (the REFUEL trial) — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: J Clin Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-20
- **Authors**: Bøhn SH et al.
- **Aims**: Examine effect of a 12-week multidisciplinary intervention on persistent fatigue among lymphoma survivors.
- **Method**: RCT; n=150 lymphoma survivors with persistent fatigue; intervention (exercise, CBT-based program, education, nutritional counseling) vs. usual care; primary outcome total fatigue (Chalder scale) at 3 months via linear mixed models.
- **Key findings**: Intervention significantly reduced fatigue at 3 months (BGD −3.5; 95% CI −5.4 to −1.6); effect maintained at 6 months but dissipated by 9 months.
- **Strengths**: Rigorous RCT with multiple follow-up timepoints; multimodal intervention addresses complex fatigue etiology.
- **Limitations**: Unblinded intervention; effect not sustained at 9 months; predominantly Norwegian sample.
- **Conclusion**: A multidisciplinary intervention reduces persistent fatigue in lymphoma survivors, with benefits lasting to 6 months post-randomization.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00414 / PMID:43063370

## 3. Cancer Symptom Severity Progression Among Adults With Severe Psychiatric Illness: A Matched Cohort Study — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: J Natl Cancer Inst
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-20
- **Authors**: Yu X et al.
- **Aims**: Investigate long-term cancer symptom trajectories (pain, nausea, wellbeing) in patients with vs. without severe psychiatric illness (SPI) history.
- **Method**: Population-based 1:5 matched cohort; Ontario cancer registry 2007–2021; Markov multistate models for symptom transitions; n=6,235 SPI + 31,175 controls.
- **Key findings**: SPI patients had accelerated pain deterioration (none-to-mild RR 1.69; mild-to-moderate RR 2.02) and 8.4% lower probability of pain improvement within 60 days.
- **Strengths**: Large population-based sample; Markov multistate modeling captures dynamic symptom transitions; long follow-up.
- **Limitations**: Administrative data may misclassify symptom severity; SPI defined by inpatient history only; unmeasured confounders.
- **Conclusion**: Patients with severe psychiatric illness face significantly worse cancer symptom trajectories, underscoring the need for tailored supportive care.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag457 / PMID:43064682

## 4. Performance of Longitudinal Cancer Screening in Li-Fraumeni Syndrome — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: J Natl Cancer Inst
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-23
- **Authors**: Khincha PP et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate performance of a multi-modal annual cancer screening protocol in Li-Fraumeni syndrome over up to 10 years.
- **Method**: Longitudinal prospective study (NCT01443468); 145 LFS participants, 772 annual visits; whole-body MRI, brain MRI, breast MRI, mammography, colonoscopy, ultrasound; GEE analysis.
- **Key findings**: 75% of cancers were screen-detected; overall sensitivity 71.7%, specificity 89%; WBMRI sensitivity 94%, NPV 99.9%.
- **Strengths**: Largest longitudinal LFS screening study; prospective registered design; comprehensive multi-modal protocol evaluation.
- **Limitations**: Single NCI centre; n=145; 25% interval cancers undetected; long-term survival outcomes not reported.
- **Conclusion**: Multi-modal LFS screening detects most cancers at early stages; WBMRI is the highest-performing modality, but interval cancers remain a challenge.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag461 / PMID:43070651

## 5. Reach of State-Mandated Insurance Coverage for Fertility Preservation in Patients With Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: J Clin Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-20
- **Authors**: Chacón KM et al.
- **Aims**: Assess real-world reach of California's fertility preservation insurance mandate and identify social determinants of eligibility among young cancer patients.
- **Method**: Retrospective cohort; n=903 patients at NCI comprehensive cancer centre 2021–2024; primary outcome FP mandate eligibility; adjusted relative risk analysis incorporating Social Vulnerability Index.
- **Key findings**: Only 18% eligible for state-mandated FP benefits; non-English speakers 64% less likely to be eligible (adjusted RR 0.36; 95% CI 0.17–0.75).
- **Strengths**: Directly quantifies policy reach; demonstrates social determinants of disparities; large consecutive series with SDOH data.
- **Limitations**: Single-centre, California only; retrospective design; may not generalise across states.
- **Conclusion**: California's FP mandate excludes 82% of at-risk cancer patients and perpetuates language- and insurance-based disparities.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-01296 / PMID:43063371

## Appraisal notes

This week's digest reflects two distinct but complementary methodological trends: rigorous interventional designs (phase II perioperative trial, RCT for survivorship fatigue) and sophisticated observational epidemiology (Markov multistate modelling for symptom trajectories, prospective longitudinal screening performance). The psychiatric illness matched cohort stands out for applying multistate modelling rarely used in cancer-epi, enabling granular capture of within-patient symptom transitions over time. The fertility preservation policy study exemplifies the growing use of health administrative and clinical data to audit real-world implementation gaps in protective legislation, with actionable disparities identified by language and insurance type.
