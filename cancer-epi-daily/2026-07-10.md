## 1. Liquid Biopsy Multi-Omics for HCC Early Detection (ASCEND-Hep) — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: BMC Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-06
- **Authors**: Wang C et al.
- **Aims**: Develop and validate a cfDNA methylation + AFP multi-omics model (MOED) for HCC early detection in high-risk populations.
- **Method**: Prospective multiphase development and validation study; training (n=635), internal validation, independent external validation (n=797), and blind community test (n=452).
- **Key findings**: MOED achieved 91.9% sensitivity and 98.4% specificity in independent validation; 92.9% sensitivity and 90.6% specificity in high-risk community screening.
- **Strengths**: Three-phase validation including blind community testing provides robust real-world performance evidence.
- **Limitations**: Recruitment from a single region (South China) limits generalizability; AFP threshold-based integration may not transfer to low-AFP populations.
- **Conclusion**: cfDNA methylation + AFP integration is highly effective for HCC screening in high-risk populations and ready for prospective evaluation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1186/s12916-026-05032-2 / PMID:42410425

## 2. GeneLLM: Transformer-Based cfRNA Liquid Biopsy for Multi-Cancer Detection — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-09
- **Authors**: Deng S et al.
- **Aims**: Enable sequence-level transformer modelling of plasma cfRNA reads for annotation-independent multi-cancer detection.
- **Method**: Transformer (GeneLLM) processing raw nucleotide sequences of cfRNA without gene-level quantification; multi-centre cohort study.
- **Key findings**: AUC 0.925–0.996 across multiple cancer types; comparable performance achieved at one-sixth of typical sequencing depth.
- **Strengths**: Captures transcriptomic dark matter from unannotated/repetitive regions; cost-efficient and scalable for population screening.
- **Limitations**: Validation cohort compositions not fully described; generalisability across ethnically diverse populations unclear.
- **Conclusion**: Sequence-level cfRNA modelling captures diagnostically relevant cancer signals beyond annotation-dependent approaches.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-74411-3 / PMID:42425994

## 3. KRAS-G12D Inhibitor HRS-4642 + Chemotherapy in Advanced PDAC: Phase 1b/2 Trial — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-09
- **Authors**: Cui J et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate safety and efficacy of liposomal KRAS-G12D inhibitor HRS-4642 combined with nab-paclitaxel + gemcitabine in advanced KRAS-mutant PDAC.
- **Method**: Phase 1b/2 trial; n=31 treated (30 treatment-naive); dose escalation with ORR as primary phase 2 endpoint; median follow-up 12.3 months.
- **Key findings**: ORR 63.3% (95% CI 43.9–80.1%) in treatment-naive patients; no dose-limiting toxicities; grade ≥3 TRAEs in 90.3%, primarily haematologic.
- **Strengths**: Novel intravenous liposomal formulation enhances tumour accumulation and avoids GI toxicity of oral KRAS inhibitors.
- **Limitations**: Small single-arm cohort (n=30 treatment-naive); no randomised comparator; long-term durability data pending.
- **Conclusion**: HRS-4642 + AG shows promising antitumour activity with manageable toxicity, supporting further randomised investigation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04538-9 / PMID:42426224

## 4. NEVA: Vision-Language AI for Precision Oncology in Neuroblastoma — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature Communications
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-09
- **Authors**: Zhu J et al.
- **Aims**: Develop NEVA, a multimodal foundation model, for neuroblastoma subtype classification, risk stratification, and molecular biomarker prediction from routine pathology slides.
- **Method**: Retrospective multi-institutional cohort (n=1,238 patients); hierarchical vision-language model with end-to-end optimisation; benchmarked against 10 foundation models across 11 tasks.
- **Key findings**: AUROC 0.916 for subtype classification, 0.924 for NMYC amplification prediction; outperformed TITAN, UNI, and Virchow on most tasks.
- **Strengths**: Largest multi-institutional neuroblastoma cohort for AI validation; interpretable attention maps for clinical decision support.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective design; model developed for a single paediatric cancer type; external generalisability to low-resource settings unclear.
- **Conclusion**: NEVA provides a scalable framework for neuroblastoma risk stratification from routine pathology data, reducing dependency on molecular profiling.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-74865-5 / PMID:42426002

## 5. Racial/Ethnic Disparities in HER2+ Metastatic Breast Cancer Treated with Antibody-Drug Conjugates — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-07
- **Authors**: Shah Z et al.
- **Aims**: Examine whether survival gains from HER2-directed ADCs (T-DXd, T-DM1) have translated equitably across racial and ethnic groups in HER2+ metastatic breast cancer.
- **Method**: Retrospective cohort study using TriNetX EHR network (2010–2024); n=7,462; Kaplan-Meier and multivariable Cox regression adjusted for comorbidities and prior therapy lines.
- **Key findings**: Black patients had significantly worse OS vs. non-Hispanic White (aHR 1.22; 95% CI 1.03–1.45; P=0.03); Asian patients had the highest 5-year OS (89.9%).
- **Strengths**: Large real-world population-level cohort; rigorous multivariable adjustment; multi-racial/ethnic comparison.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective EHR data with potential residual confounding; limited granularity on ADC sequencing, dose intensity, and socioeconomic factors.
- **Conclusion**: Racial disparities in HER2+ MBC persist despite modern ADC therapies, underscoring the need for equity-focused interventions.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag223 / PMID:42411768
