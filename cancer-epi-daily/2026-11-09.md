## 1. In vivo CAR-T double knockout screen maps synergistic antitumour gene pairs — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-04
- **Authors**: Zhou L et al.
- **Aims**: Identify synergistic gene-pair knockouts that enhance CAR-T cell anti-tumour efficacy.
- **Method**: High-throughput in vivo CRISPR DKO screen in CAR-T cells, validated by scRNA-seq and multiple tumour models.
- **Key findings**: NR4A1-SOCS3 DKO top-ranked pair; conferred superior metabolic fitness and mitochondrial activity. Outperformed single-gene and dual-gene benchmarks across solid and haematologic tumour models without safety compromise.
- **Strengths**: First large-scale in vivo CAR-T DKO screen; mechanistically validated with whole-transcriptome profiling and scRNA-seq.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical only; translation to human CAR-T products and patient heterogeneity not yet assessed.
- **Conclusion**: NR4A1-SOCS3 is a promising intracellular checkpoint pair for engineering high-performance CAR-T therapies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10956-z / PMID:42983097

## 2. Circulating Tumor DNA Profiling Across Phase II/III Ewing Sarcoma Trials Identifies High-Risk Patients — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-04
- **Authors**: Mous L et al.
- **Aims**: Prospectively characterise ctDNA landscape and prognostic value in Ewing sarcoma trials.
- **Method**: Prospective serial ctDNA sampling (991 samples, 347 patients) across Euro Ewing 2012 and CombinaiR3 trials using targeted sequencing and shallow WGS.
- **Key findings**: TP53/CDKN2A alterations independently defined a molecular high-risk subgroup with inferior EFS (HR 3.6, p<0.001) and OS (HR 4.3, p<0.001) after multivariable adjustment. ctDNA revealed intrapatient heterogeneity and clonal evolution at relapse.
- **Strengths**: First prospective, trial-integrated ctDNA profiling in Ewing sarcoma; large multinational cohort with rigorous multivariable modelling.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective analysis of prospective trial data; targeted panels may miss novel alterations.
- **Conclusion**: ctDNA-guided risk stratification is feasible and independently prognostic in Ewing sarcoma.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00280 / PMID:42981545

## 3. Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substance Exposure and Breast Cancer Incidence in the Sister Study — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-05
- **Authors**: Chang CJ et al.
- **Aims**: Examine prospective associations of 17 PFAS with breast cancer incidence by hormone receptor status.
- **Method**: Case-cohort study within the Sister Study (1,609 cases, 2,269 subcohort); prediagnostic plasma PFAS measured by LC-HRMS; sampling-weighted Cox models; meta-analysis of 7 studies.
- **Key findings**: PFOA (HR 1.67, 95%CI 1.10–2.53) and PFOS (HR 1.93, 95%CI 1.15–3.22) associated with ER-negative breast cancer. Meta-analysis confirmed PFOA RR 1.30 (95%CI 1.05–1.62) for ER-negative tumours.
- **Strengths**: Prediagnostic biomarkers, 17 PFAS quantified, oversampling of ER-negative cases, supporting meta-analysis.
- **Limitations**: Residual confounding cannot be excluded; PFAS co-exposures limit isolating individual compound effects.
- **Conclusion**: PFOA and PFOS may increase ER-negative breast cancer risk; findings have important public health implications given widespread PFAS exposure.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00318 / PMID:42987247

## 4. International Initiative on a Refined Staging System for Medullary Thyroid Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-04
- **Authors**: Shi X et al.
- **Aims**: Develop an improved prognostic staging system for medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) over AJCC TNM-8.
- **Method**: Retrospective multicenter study (2,573 patients, 43 centers, 15 countries); exhaustive enumeration of 116,284 TNM stage-grouping schemas via Cox modelling; validated in SEER cohort (N=1,909).
- **Key findings**: Proposed system showed monotonic risk increase across stages (II–IV aHRs 2.22, 4.81, 15.75; all p≤0.001) vs poor discrimination in TNM-8. C-index improved from 0.856 to 0.892 (p<0.001).
- **Strengths**: Largest multinational MTC cohort; systematic exhaustive search of all admissible staging schemas; independent SEER validation.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective design; some centers had limited follow-up; RET mutation status not uniformly available.
- **Conclusion**: The proposed IMTCSC staging system substantially improves prognostic discrimination over AJCC TNM-8 for MTC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-01146 / PMID:42981561

## 5. Molecular Testing Among Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer in the Rural US — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Network Open
- **Pub date**: 2026-11-02
- **Authors**: Rhinehart DP et al.
- **Aims**: Characterise molecular testing uptake and predictors in rural Medicare patients with advanced NSCLC.
- **Method**: SEER-Medicare linked cohort (8,259 rural beneficiaries ≥66 years, 2014–2019); competing-risks cumulative incidence with death as competing event.
- **Key findings**: Only 35.9% received molecular testing. Dual Medicaid coverage reduced testing (HR 0.71, 95%CI 0.64–0.79); joint proceduralist and oncologist visits strongly associated with testing (77.7% vs 58.9%).
- **Strengths**: Large, nationally representative real-world dataset; competing-risks framework appropriately accounts for mortality; identifies actionable access predictors.
- **Limitations**: Claims-based ascertainment may miss some tests; older Medicare cohort limits generalisability to younger patients.
- **Conclusion**: Guideline-concordant molecular testing remains low in rural advanced NSCLC patients; dual coverage and lack of subspecialist access are key equity barriers.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.42270 / PMID:42986646
