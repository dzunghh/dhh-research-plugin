## 1. Colonoscopy Intervals and Colorectal Cancer Incidence after Adenoma Removal — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-17
- **Authors**: Jover R et al.
- **Aims**: To determine whether beginning colonoscopy surveillance 5 years after polyp removal is noninferior to the guideline-recommended 3-year interval.
- **Method**: Multicenter noninferiority RCT in 8 European countries (n=10,799 high-risk adenoma patients); primary endpoint cumulative CRC incidence at 10 years; 5.5-year interim analysis with inverse probability weighting.
- **Key findings**: 5-year cumulative CRC incidence 0.77% (5-yr group) vs 0.82% (3-yr group); upper CI boundary 0.68 met noninferiority margin of 0.7 pp.
- **Strengths**: Large, multicenter RCT with pre-specified noninferiority margin and rigorous missing-data handling via IPW.
- **Limitations**: Interim analysis only; 10-year primary endpoint not yet reached; compliance with surveillance not universal.
- **Conclusion**: A 5-year first surveillance colonoscopy is noninferior to 3 years for CRC prevention in high-risk adenoma patients at 5.5 years.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2603816 / PMID:42748427

## 2. Liquid biopsy for early detection of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-16
- **Authors**: Xu C et al.
- **Aims**: To develop and validate a blood-based microRNA assay (PANXEON) for detecting early-stage PDAC.
- **Method**: International multicenter prospective observational biomarker study (n=1,785 individuals, 4 countries); 10-miRNA signature integrated with CA19-9 into a composite score.
- **Key findings**: Testing cohort AUC 88.6%; sensitivity 83.8% for early-stage PDAC; combined PANXEON sensitivity 86.8% for stage I–II with FPR 3.2% in low-risk controls.
- **Strengths**: Large international prospective cohort with multi-stage validation and monitoring of treatment-related biomarker dynamics.
- **Limitations**: False-positive rate 15.6% in high-risk controls; requires prospective large-scale studies before clinical implementation.
- **Conclusion**: PANXEON miRNA+CA19-9 composite score shows clinically promising sensitivity for stage I–II PDAC and warrants further prospective evaluation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04625-x / PMID:42749887

## 3. Chemotherapy-free phase 1/2 trial of HRS-4642 plus adebrelimab in previously treated metastatic KRAS G12D-mutant pancreatic cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Cell
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-16
- **Authors**: Shi S et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate the safety and efficacy of KRAS G12D inhibitor HRS-4642 combined with PD-L1 blockade adebrelimab in pretreated metastatic PDAC.
- **Method**: Phase 1/2 trial (NCT06427239); dose escalation in 48 patients with KRAS G12D-mutant PDAC; chemotherapy-free regimen.
- **Key findings**: ORR 43.2% (95% CI 27.1–60.5); median OS 11.5 months; median PFS 5.7 months in RP2D cohort (n=37); no dose-limiting toxicities.
- **Strengths**: First chemotherapy-free combination strategy targeting KRAS G12D in PDAC, with mechanistically rational PD-L1 co-blockade.
- **Limitations**: Small single-arm cohort; no direct comparison arm; heavily pretreated population limits generalizability.
- **Conclusion**: HRS-4642 plus adebrelimab demonstrates manageable toxicity and encouraging antitumor activity in refractory KRAS G12D-mutant PDAC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.08.012 / PMID:42748921

## 4. Blinatumomab for Replacing Chemotherapy in Pediatric Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-17
- **Authors**: Schrappe M et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate whether blinatumomab can safely replace two cycles of conventional chemotherapy in newly diagnosed high-risk B-cell ALL in children.
- **Method**: Phase 3 multicenter RCT (n=709; 1:1 randomization); interim analysis at median 2.9 years follow-up; primary endpoint 4-year event-free survival.
- **Key findings**: 4-year EFS 83.0% blinatumomab vs 70.3% control (HR 0.51, 95% CI 0.35–0.73); infection rates 23.9% vs 69.4%.
- **Strengths**: Large international Phase 3 RCT with pre-planned interim, demonstrating both efficacy gain and substantial reduction in chemotherapy toxicity.
- **Limitations**: Interim analysis; longer follow-up needed; neurotoxicity higher in blinatumomab arm (12.0% vs 3.2%).
- **Conclusion**: Replacing two chemotherapy cycles with blinatumomab significantly improves EFS and reduces serious infections in high-risk pediatric B-cell ALL.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2604166 / PMID:42748428

## 5. Oral KRAS G12D inhibitor GFH375 for previously treated advanced solid tumors with KRAS mutations: a phase 1 trial — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nat Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-15
- **Authors**: Ai X et al.
- **Aims**: To assess safety, tolerability, and antitumor activity of GFH375, a dual ON/OFF state KRAS G12D inhibitor, in KRAS-mutant advanced solid tumors.
- **Method**: Phase 1 dose-escalation trial (NCT06500676; n=74); oral GFH375 once or twice daily; primary endpoints safety and RP2D.
- **Key findings**: RP2D 600 mg QD; confirmed ORR 35.1% in PDAC (n=37) and 35.7% in NSCLC (n=14); grade ≥3 TRAEs in 36.5%.
- **Strengths**: Novel dual ON/OFF KRAS inhibitor mechanism with ORR comparable to other KRAS inhibitors; covers PDAC, NSCLC, CRC cohorts.
- **Limitations**: Single-country Phase 1; no randomized comparison; short median DOR (~5.6 months); small NSCLC/CRC cohorts.
- **Conclusion**: GFH375 shows manageable safety and clinically meaningful antitumor activity across KRAS G12D-mutant solid tumors, supporting Phase 2 expansion.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41591-026-04559-4 / PMID:42745055
