## 1. Phase 1 Study of Anito-cel, a d-Domain BCMA CAR T Cell for Refractory or Recurrent Myeloma — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-24
- **Authors**: Frigault MJ et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate safety and efficacy of anito-cel, a novel synthetic d-domain BCMA CAR T-cell therapy, in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma.
- **Method**: Phase 1 multicenter clinical trial (n=38); two dose levels; median follow-up 38.1 months; primary endpoints adverse events and recommended phase 2 dose.
- **Key findings**: 100% overall response rate; 79% complete response; 24-month PFS 57%; median PFS 30.2 months; grade ≥3 CRS absent at recommended dose.
- **Strengths**: Long follow-up (38.1 months), novel binder mechanism reducing off-target toxicity, 100% response rate in heavily pretreated patients.
- **Limitations**: Phase 1, single-arm, small sample (n=38); no comparator arm; sponsor-funded trial.
- **Conclusion**: Anito-cel shows exceptional efficacy with manageable toxicity, warranting phase 2/3 evaluation in myeloma.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMoa2603527 / PMID:42777241

## 2. Causal Inference of Multiple Interrelated Exposures: Contributions of Ischemic Placental Disease to Preterm Delivery — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Am J Epidemiol
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-23
- **Authors**: Loh WW et al.
- **Aims**: Estimate composite and separate causal effects of ischemic placental disease (IPD) conditions on spontaneous and indicated preterm delivery using multi-exposure causal inference.
- **Method**: Nested cohort (Consortium on Safe Labor, n=203,990); joint stochastic/deterministic interventions specified; targeted maximum likelihood estimation applied.
- **Key findings**: Eliminating all IPD reduced indicated PTD (RR=0.66); preeclampsia alone drove the largest iPTD reduction (RR=0.72); aspirin-level reduction yielded modest iPTD reduction (RR=0.89).
- **Strengths**: Novel multi-X causal inference framework for joint exposures; very large nationally representative dataset.
- **Limitations**: Causal assumptions (positivity, no unmeasured confounding) may not fully hold; retrospective data.
- **Conclusion**: Multi-exposure causal inference framework demonstrates preeclampsia as the dominant IPD driver of indicated preterm delivery.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/aje/kwag230 / PMID:42773779

## 3. Contemporary Menopausal Hormone Therapy and Thrombotic Disease: Nationwide Nested Case-Control Study — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: BMJ
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-23
- **Authors**: Berggreen J et al.
- **Aims**: Investigate associations between menopausal hormone therapy formulations and risk of venous thromboembolism, ischemic stroke, and myocardial infarction.
- **Method**: Nationwide nested case-control study; Danish national health registries; 9,807 VTE, 18,460 stroke, 11,974 MI cases; matched controls; 2003–2021.
- **Key findings**: Oral oestrogen associated with increased VTE (HR 1.6), ischaemic stroke (HR 1.3), MI (HR 1.2); transdermal therapy showed no increased thrombotic risk overall.
- **Strengths**: National registry data eliminates recall bias; very large sample; distinguishes route, dose, and duration systematically.
- **Limitations**: Observational design; confounding by indication possible; cancer patients excluded limits generalizability.
- **Conclusion**: Oral but not transdermal menopausal HRT is associated with increased thrombotic risk; findings support transdermal preference for at-risk women.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1136/bmj-2026-100688 / PMID:42778212

## 4. Fast, Flexible Analysis of Differences in Cellular Composition with crumblr — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nat Commun
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-23
- **Authors**: Hoffman GE et al.
- **Aims**: Introduce crumblr, a scalable statistical method for compositional single-cell RNA-seq data analysis incorporating complex study designs.
- **Method**: Precision-weighted linear mixed models on count ratio data; multivariate cell lineage hierarchy testing; evaluated by simulation and applied to four published scRNA-seq datasets including prostate cancer bone metastases.
- **Key findings**: crumblr increases statistical power over existing methods while controlling false positive rate; open-source software (GitHub released).
- **Strengths**: Novel multivariate approach across cell lineage hierarchy; validated by simulation; open-source tool with cancer application demonstrated.
- **Limitations**: Requires single-cell data with cell type annotations; mixed model assumptions may not hold in all designs.
- **Conclusion**: crumblr provides a principled, scalable framework for cell composition analysis in large single-cell cohorts including cancer studies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75681-7 / PMID:42778538

- **Journal**: Nat Rev Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-22
- **Authors**: Chen J et al.
- **Aims**: Review molecular mechanisms of replication stress in cancer and the therapeutic vulnerabilities it creates.
- **Method**: Comprehensive narrative review; covers ATR-CHK1 pathway, PARP inhibition synthetic lethality, WRN inhibition in MSI cancers, and clinical pipeline for replication stress inhibitors.

- **Limitations**: Narrative review; clinical translation of ATR/CHK1/WEE1 inhibitors remains early-stage.
- **Conclusion**: Replication stress is both a cancer driver and a precision oncology target, with clinical inhibitor development advancing.
