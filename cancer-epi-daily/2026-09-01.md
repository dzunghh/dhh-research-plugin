## 1. Long-Term Survival Outcomes of Modified-FOLFOXIRI Plus Cetuximab Versus Bevacizumab in Wild-Type Metastatic Colorectal Cancer: Final Analysis of the DEEPER Trial — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-27
- **Authors**: Sunakawa Y et al.
- **Aims**: Report final OS/PFS outcomes of m-FOLFOXIRI + cetuximab vs bevacizumab in RAS/BRAF wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer.
- **Method**: Randomised phase II trial (DEEPER); per-protocol survival analysis with exploratory subgroup analyses by tumour sidedness, liver-limited disease, and sex.
- **Key findings**: In RAS/BRAF wild-type left-sided tumours, cetuximab arm had superior PFS (14.8 vs 11.9 months; HR 0.71). Exploratory OS benefit with cetuximab in male patients (HR 0.59) and extrahepatic disease (HR 0.60).
- **Strengths**: Randomised design; mature follow-up enabling final OS analysis; pre-specified subgroup of left-sided tumours.
- **Limitations**: Phase II; exploratory subgroup analyses underpowered; no overall OS difference in the full per-protocol set.
- **Conclusion**: m-FOLFOXIRI plus cetuximab may offer superior outcomes in RAS/BRAF wild-type, left-sided mCRC, warranting confirmatory phase III evaluation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-26-00355 / PMID:42659588

## 2. Systemic Therapy Considerations in Older Adults With Early-Stage Breast Cancer: A Review — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: JAMA Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-08-27
- **Authors**: Smith CE et al.
- **Aims**: Synthesise evidence for systemic treatment of early-stage breast cancer in older adults, addressing subtype-specific strategies and decision-making tools.
- **Method**: Narrative review covering clinical trial data and real-world evidence; incorporates geriatric assessment tools for treatment personalisation.
- **Key findings**: Evidence supports adjuvant CDK4/6 inhibitors in HR+ disease and neoadjuvant pembrolizumab in TNBC; older adults remain underrepresented in trials.
- **Strengths**: Clinically actionable framework covering all breast cancer subtypes; highlights ongoing trials targeting older adults specifically.
- **Limitations**: Narrative rather than systematic review; limited randomised data specifically for patients ≥70 years.
- **Conclusion**: Clinicians should balance overtreatment risk against breast cancer mortality, guided by geriatric assessment and available subtype-specific evidence.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3121 / PMID:42658515
