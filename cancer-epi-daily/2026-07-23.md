## 1. Association between smoking habits and breast density, changes in breast density, and breast cancer in Asian women — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Journal of the National Cancer Institute
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-21
- **Authors**: Park B et al.
- **Aims**: Examine associations among smoking status, breast density, density changes, and subsequent breast cancer risk in Asian women.
- **Method**: Nationwide population-based cohort study; 4.7 million Korean women ≥40 years screened 2009–2010; BC incidence via NHIS-NHID registry through Dec 2023.
- **Key findings**: Current smokers had lower odds of dense breasts (OR 0.88, 95% CI 0.87–0.89) yet higher BC risk, especially postmenopausal; adjusting for density did not attenuate smoking–BC associations.
- **Strengths**: Exceptionally large registry-linked cohort (4.7M women) with long follow-up and dose-response analysis for smoking.
- **Limitations**: Breast density categorised in only four broad BI-RADS groups; residual confounding from unmeasured lifestyle factors possible.
- **Conclusion**: Smoking raises breast cancer risk independently of breast density, implying carcinogenic mechanisms beyond density-mediated pathways.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/jnci/djag231 / PMID:42479205

## 2. A Multimodal Deep Learning Model for Preoperative Prediction of Postoperative Complications in Gastric Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Annals of Oncology
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-16
- **Authors**: Ding PA et al.
- **Aims**: Develop and validate a multimodal DL model (DeepComp) to preoperatively predict Clavien-Dindo ≥II complications and overall survival in gastric cancer.
- **Method**: Multicenter study of 5,237 patients at 11 Chinese centers; DeepComp integrates clinical variables, CT image features, and L3 body-composition via dual-task architecture.
- **Key findings**: DeepComp AUC 0.888 (internal) and 0.824–0.869 (9 external cohorts); 15.3 pp improvement over best clinical baseline (P<0.001); surgeon sensitivity rose from 47.1% to 87.9% with tool assistance.
- **Strengths**: Extensive external validation across nine cohorts and three neoadjuvant settings; target trial emulation quantifies real-world management impact.
- **Limitations**: All validation cohorts are Chinese; generalisability to non-Asian populations is uncertain.
- **Conclusion**: DeepComp provides accurate preoperative risk stratification to guide individualised perioperative management for gastric cancer patients.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/j.annonc.2026.07.004 / PMID:42463045

## 3. Sex‑biased immunity: the hidden variable in immuno‑oncology — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-21
- **Authors**: Brown K et al.
- **Aims**: Review how biological sex differences in immune responses shape anti-tumour immunity and immunotherapy efficacy.
- **Method**: Narrative review synthesising clinical and preclinical evidence on sex as a biological variable in cancer immunotherapy, informed by NIH mandate changes.
- **Key findings**: Male and female immune systems mount measurably different anti-tumour responses; sex-stratified reporting has increased substantially since NIH mandate.
- **Strengths**: Timely consolidation of an emerging evidence base; links NIH policy changes to clinical implications for trial design.
- **Limitations**: Narrative rather than systematic review; mechanistic pathways remain incompletely characterised.
- **Conclusion**: Sex must be incorporated as a biological variable in immunotherapy trials and patient stratification frameworks.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-24-1223 / PMID:42479171

## 4. Systematic review and meta-analysis of lung metastases incidence in cervical cancer patients — Score 3/6

- **Journal**: BMC Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-21
- **Authors**: Pakdaman K et al.
- **Aims**: Estimate global incidence of lung metastases (LMs) in cervical cancer and characterise clinical and pathological patterns.
- **Method**: Systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis; multi-database search (PubMed, Scopus, Embase, WoS) through May 2025; JBI quality assessment.
- **Key findings**: Pooled LMs incidence 4.31% (95% CI 3.25–5.37); neuroendocrine carcinoma highest at 16.91%; stage IV incidence 15.75% vs 3.12% at stage I.
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive multi-database search with regional and histology-specific subgroup analyses; Africa-specific estimate provided.
- **Limitations**: Substantial heterogeneity across included studies; some regional subgroups have small sample sizes.
- **Conclusion**: Lung metastasis risk in cervical cancer is strongly patterned by histological subtype and stage, informing surveillance strategies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1186/s12885-026-16579-x / PMID:42477627
