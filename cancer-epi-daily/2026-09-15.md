## 1. Integrated signatures define mutational processes in prostate cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Gruber AJ et al.
- **Aims**: Identify integrated mutational footprints (IMFs) shaping prostate cancer genomes and assess their clinical and therapeutic relevance.
- **Method**: Large cohort study (959 donors, Pan Prostate Cancer Group); integrated de novo extraction of single-base substitution, indel, copy-number, and complex structural variant signatures.
- **Key findings**: Eight IMFs explain mutational processes in 85% of primary prostate cancers; four IMFs (37% of tumours) significantly associated with shorter time to metastasis. IMFs predicted androgen receptor pathway inhibitor sensitivity in the metastatic setting.
- **Strengths**: Large multi-centre cohort, multi-omics integration across mutation types, direct linkage to clinical outcomes and ancestry.
- **Limitations**: Cohort may under-represent non-European ancestries despite finding non-canonical HRD enriched in African-ancestry patients.
- **Conclusion**: IMFs unify aetiology and clinical heterogeneity of prostate cancer, with potential to improve risk stratification and biomarker-guided treatment.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10468-w / PMID:42717097

## 2. PLA2G2D in tumour-draining lymph nodes regulates anti-tumour immunity — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: van Krimpen A et al.
- **Aims**: Identify immunosuppressive mechanisms in tumour-draining lymph nodes (TDLNs) linked to melanoma metastasis development.
- **Method**: Imaging mass cytometry and targeted spatial transcriptomics of TDLNs from melanoma patients; genetic and antibody-mediated inhibition validated in mouse and humanised mouse models.
- **Key findings**: PLA2G2D-expressing macrophages in TDLN paracortex suppress CD8+ T cell proliferation; PLA2G2D inhibition plus anti-PD-1 showed additive/synergistic efficacy in humanised mice.
- **Strengths**: Human tissue spatial profiling combined with mechanistic mouse validation and therapeutic antibody development.
- **Limitations**: Humanised mouse models may not fully recapitulate the human immune microenvironment.
- **Conclusion**: PLA2G2D is a non-redundant TDLN-centred immune checkpoint and a promising immunotherapy target in cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10954-1 / PMID:42717091

## 3. A serpin-myeloid axis in pancreatic cancer heterogeneity and immune evasion — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Falcomatà C et al.
- **Aims**: Determine how gene-driven local immune neighbourhood remodelling underpins spatial clonal dominance and immune evasion in pancreatic ductal carcinoma (PDAC).
- **Method**: Perturb-map spatial functional genomics in PDAC; multimodal spatial profiling of patient tumours validated with mouse models and pharmacological inhibition.
- **Key findings**: SERPINE1/SERPINB2 stabilise fibrin-rich niches that retain immunosuppressive SPP1+/MARCO+ macrophages and exclude cytotoxic T cells; PAI1 inhibition or CD18 blockade synergises with anti-PD-1 in mice.
- **Strengths**: Novel Perturb-map approach linking genotype to spatial tumour microenvironment; findings validated in both mouse models and patient tumours.
- **Limitations**: Therapeutic results are preclinical; clinical translation remains to be established.
- **Conclusion**: SERPINE1/SERPINB2 are spatial organisers of immune suppression linking tumour heterogeneity to immunotherapy resistance in PDAC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-11002-8 / PMID:42717081

## 4. An operational perturbation proteomics-based virtual cell model — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-09
- **Authors**: Sun R et al.
- **Aims**: Develop an AI virtual cell model for breast cancer in silico drug discovery using large-scale temporal perturbation proteomics.
- **Method**: Generated >38 million temporal protein measurements from systematically perturbed breast cancer cell lines; developed ProteinTalks using pretraining on dynamic proteomic trajectories.
- **Key findings**: ProteinTalks predicts drug efficacy and synergy with higher performance than benchmark models; transfers robustly to patient-derived organoids and clinical biopsies.
- **Strengths**: Unprecedented temporal proteomics scale; generalises beyond cell lines to clinical specimens.
- **Limitations**: Primarily validated in breast cancer; applicability to other tumour types requires further study.
- **Conclusion**: Scalable dynamic representation pretraining enables operational virtual cell models to advance breast cancer drug discovery.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-11001-9 / PMID:42717098

## 5. Treatment Decisions in Multiple Myeloma — Score 2/6

- **Journal**: N Engl J Med
- **Pub date**: 2026-09-10
- **Authors**: Mouhieddine TH et al.
- **Aims**: Provide a practical evidence-based framework for navigating treatment decisions across the multiple myeloma disease spectrum.
- **Method**: Systematic narrative review synthesising trial data on induction, transplantation, CAR-T, bispecific antibodies, and early interception in smoldering myeloma.
- **Key findings**: Quadruplet induction is now standard; autologous transplantation role is being reevaluated; CAR-T and bispecific antibody sequencing depends on T-cell fitness and multiantigen targeting strategies.
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive integration of genomic risk stratification, measurable residual disease, and functional fitness for individualised decisions.
- **Limitations**: Rapidly evolving field; some recommendations may be superseded by ongoing trials.
- **Conclusion**: Functional cure is an emerging goal in multiple myeloma, achievable through risk-stratified, biomarker-guided treatment selection.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1056/NEJMra2605253 / PMID:42715563
