## 1. Neoadjuvant Toripalimab Plus Celecoxib vs Toripalimab Monotherapy for dMMR/MSI-H Locally Advanced CRC (PICC-2) — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Lancet Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-01
- **Authors**: Hu H et al.
- **Aims**: To determine whether adding COX-2 inhibitor celecoxib to neoadjuvant toripalimab improves pathological complete response in dMMR/MSI-H locally advanced colorectal cancer.
- **Method**: Open-label, multicentre, phase 2 RCT (n=110) across three Chinese hospitals; randomised 1:1 to toripalimab+celecoxib vs toripalimab alone for 12 cycles pre-surgery.
- **Key findings**: pCR 89% (95% CI 78–96%) vs 69% (55–81%) in monotherapy arm; between-group difference 19 pp (95% CI 4–34; p=0.014).
- **Strengths**: Central blinded pathological review, pre-registered, high completion rate (89% per arm), meaningful absolute pCR gain.
- **Limitations**: Phase 2 only, all-Chinese cohort, no long-term survival data at cutoff.
- **Conclusion**: COX-2 inhibition synergises with PD-1 blockade in dMMR CRC neoadjuvant setting, warranting phase 3 evaluation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1016/S1470-2045(26)00220-2 / PMID:42385761

## 2. TROP2 Targeting Reveals Therapy-Driven Cell State Dynamics in Colorectal Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-01
- **Authors**: Vaquero-Siguero N et al.
- **Aims**: To characterise TROP2 as a marker of poor-prognosis WNT/fetal-like CRC states and assess antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) targeting.
- **Method**: Multi-model translational study using patient-derived organoids, mouse models, and clinical datasets; time-resolved cell-state profiling under TROP2-directed ADC vs chemotherapy.
- **Key findings**: TROP2+ cells represent chemotherapy-induced fetal-like states; TROP2-ADC selectively depletes these, and combined ADC+chemotherapy enhances efficacy in patient-derived models.
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive mechanistic-to-translational pipeline, clinically actionable target with FDA-approved class agent.
- **Limitations**: Primarily preclinical; clinical trial validation not yet reported.
- **Conclusion**: TROP2 marks a therapy-resistant CRC state that can be exploited by ADC combination strategies.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10705-2 / PMID:42386981

## 3. Casdatifan Shows Durable Response Linked to HIF-2α Biology in Kidney Cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-01
- **Authors**: Choueiri TK et al.
- **Aims**: To report efficacy and safety of casdatifan (HIF-2α inhibitor) in refractory metastatic clear cell renal cell carcinoma.
- **Method**: Dose-expansion cohort from phase I/II ARC-20 study; 100 mg QD cohort (n=32) and total cohort (n=127) with biomarker correlates.
- **Key findings**: Confirmed ORR 35% (100 mg QD) and 31% (total); median PFS not reached (100 mg QD) and 12.2 months (total); erythropoietin reduction correlated with clinical benefit.
- **Strengths**: Robust biomarker-clinical outcome linkage; manageable safety (only 3% discontinuation).
- **Limitations**: Non-randomised dose-expansion design; no comparator arm.
- **Conclusion**: Casdatifan produces durable, HIF-2α pathway-linked responses in refractory ccRCC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10718-x / PMID:42386974

## 4. Robotic Versus Open Pancreatoduodenectomy (PORTAL): Phase 3 Non-Inferiority RCT — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: BMJ
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-01
- **Authors**: Jin J et al.
- **Aims**: To determine whether robotic pancreatoduodenectomy (RPD) is non-inferior to open pancreatoduodenectomy (OPD) for postoperative functional recovery.
- **Method**: Phase 3, single-masked, multicentre non-inferiority RCT at seven Chinese tertiary pancreatic centres (n=268); restricted mean event time within 40 days as primary endpoint.
- **Key findings**: RMET 12.1 days (RPD) vs 16.0 days (OPD), difference −3.9 days (95% CI −5.6 to −2.2; p<0.001); Clavien-Dindo ≥II complications 23.5% vs 34.4%.
- **Strengths**: First phase 3 RCT for this comparison; standardised surgical protocols; pre-specified non-inferiority margin.
- **Limitations**: High-volume Chinese centres; RPD was costlier; longer operative time in RPD arm.
- **Conclusion**: RPD meets non-inferiority for functional recovery and reduces major complications at expert centres, though higher costs and operative time remain.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1136/bmj-2026-319692 / PMID:42386316

## 5. Efficacy of Zenocutuzumab in Advanced NRG1 Fusion-Positive Cholangiocarcinoma (eNRGy Phase II) — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: J Clin Oncol
- **Pub date**: 2026-07-01
- **Authors**: Cleary JM et al.
- **Aims**: To evaluate zenocutuzumab (HER2×HER3 bispecific antibody) in NRG1 fusion-positive (NRG1+) cholangiocarcinoma.
- **Method**: Single-arm phase II study (eNRGy trial); n=22 NRG1+ cholangiocarcinoma patients; primary endpoint investigator-assessed ORR (RECIST v1.1).
- **Key findings**: ORR 36.8% (95% CI 16.3–61.6%) in 19 evaluable patients; median DOR 7.4 months; median PFS 9.2 months; no grade ≥4 treatment-related AEs.
- **Strengths**: First targeted therapy data in NRG1+ cholangiocarcinoma; durable responses; favourable safety.
- **Limitations**: Very small evaluable n=19, single arm, no comparator; exclusively intrahepatic subtype.
- **Conclusion**: Zenocutuzumab shows clinically meaningful, durable activity in this molecularly defined cholangiocarcinoma subset.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1200/JCO-25-03128 / PMID:42385125

## Appraisal notes

This week's digest is dominated by high-quality interventional oncology, with two phase 3/randomised controlled trials (PICC-2 and PORTAL) and two phase I/II dose-expansion studies, reflecting continued refinement of precision and surgical oncology rather than classic epidemiological designs. The PICC-2 trial is the standout finding — achieving 89% pCR with COX-2 augmentation of immune checkpoint blockade in dMMR CRC suggests tumour microenvironment modulation as a scalable neoadjuvant strategy. Methodologically, the use of restricted mean event time (PORTAL trial) and central blinded pathological review (PICC-2) represent improving analytical rigour in surgical and oncological RCTs.
