## 1. Switches in alternatively spliced isoforms impact protein and drug interactions in melanoma, ovarian cancer, and AML — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-03
- **Authors**: Karakulak T et al.
- **Aims**: Identify and validate most-dominant transcript (MDT) isoform switches in melanoma, ovarian cancer, and AML versus normal tissue as candidate biomarkers and therapeutic targets.
- **Method**: Multi-cohort transcriptomic analysis of 6,114 cancer-associated MDTs (cMDTs) with proteomics validation and protein/drug interaction network analysis.
- **Key findings**: 1,707 cMDTs encode transmembrane proteins; 12.1% validated by mass spectrometry. Most cMDTs lose protein partner and drug compound interactions and correlate with spliceosome switches.
- **Strengths**: Largest validated cMDT study to date across three cancer types; integrates proteomics for validation.
- **Limitations**: Cross-sectional design; clinical outcome associations not tested.
- **Conclusion**: Cancer-associated isoform switches represent novel diagnostic biomarker candidates with implications for drug resistance.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03638-0 / PMID:42829369

## 2. Endotrophin is a circulating fibroinflammatory biomarker associated with treatment response in human metastatic ER+ breast cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-05
- **Authors**: Santos B et al.
- **Aims**: Assess circulating endotrophin (ETP) levels as a predictive/prognostic biomarker across breast cancer subtypes and treatment settings.
- **Method**: Observational biomarker study; 191 samples from 98 patients (ER+, TNBC) plus ovarian and healthy controls; ELISA-based circulating ETP measurement.
- **Key findings**: ER+/HER2- tumors had higher ETP (median 35.98 ng/mL) vs TNBC (19.67 ng/mL); ETP associated with treatment response only in metastatic setting.
- **Strengths**: Multiple comparison groups including healthy controls; both tissue and circulating ETP assessed.
- **Limitations**: Single-institution; relatively small sample; no prospective validation cohort.
- **Conclusion**: Circulating ETP is a candidate biomarker for ER+ metastatic breast cancer treatment monitoring.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-25-3775 / PMID:42832372

## 3. Abundant CD8+ tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes in glioblastoma harbor distinct exhaustion signatures in potentially reactive subsets — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: British Journal of Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-05
- **Authors**: Okamoto T et al.
- **Aims**: Identify tumor-specific T cells in glioblastoma (GBM) to inform antigen-specific immunotherapy strategies.
- **Method**: Single-cell RNA and TCR sequencing of CD8+ TILs from 56 IDH-wildtype GBM patients; AI-based reactive TCR prediction; comparison with public TIL datasets.
- **Key findings**: Dominant TIL cluster expresses high LAG3/CXCL13; AI algorithm identified markedly higher potentially reactive T-cell proportion vs published GBM and lung cancer datasets.
- **Strengths**: Multi-dataset validation; integrates single-cell transcriptomics with TCR repertoire analysis.
- **Limitations**: Single-center Japanese cohort; functional validation of predicted reactive T cells not performed.
- **Conclusion**: LAG3-high clonally expanded TILs in GBM may include reactive subsets targetable by antigen-specific or LAG3-directed therapy.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03619-3 / PMID:42834163

## 4. Topoisomerase-Based CDH17 Antibody Drug Conjugates Overcome P-gp Resistance but Undergo Target Mediated Clearance in Colorectal Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-05
- **Authors**: O'Brien NA et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate payload selection and target-mediated clearance effects on CDH17-directed ADCs in colorectal cancer.
- **Method**: Preclinical study comparing MMAE vs exatecan-payload CDH17 ADCs in CRC cell lines with variable P-gp expression; IHC for CDH17 expression; PK studies in cynomolgus monkeys and human CDH17 knock-in mice.
- **Key findings**: Exatecan-payload ADC overcame P-gp-induced resistance; CDH17-directed antibodies showed ~4-fold faster clearance in human CDH17-expressing mice, indicating substantial target-mediated drug disposition.
- **Strengths**: Multiple models (in vitro, in vivo); clinically relevant PK assessment with humanized animal model.
- **Limitations**: Preclinical only; translational relevance to human dosing uncertain.
- **Conclusion**: Exatecan payload is preferred for P-gp-expressing CRCs, but target-mediated clearance is a key translational challenge.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-2010 / PMID:42832380

## 5. Progress in Clinical Care and Research for Li-Fraumeni Syndrome — Score 3/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-05
- **Authors**: MacFarland SP et al.
- **Aims**: Summarize advances and identify research priorities for Li-Fraumeni Syndrome (LFS) from the 7th REACH Symposium.
- **Method**: Expert consensus review covering p53 biology, TP53 variant classification, genotype-phenotype correlations, cancer spectrum, psychosocial impact, and screening.
- **Key findings**: Five priority research areas identified; current gaps include precise TP53 variant classification and practical cancer screening methods; psychosocial burden significant.

- **Conclusion**: LFS management requires improved variant interpretation, cancer risk prediction tools, and novel prevention/interception strategies.
