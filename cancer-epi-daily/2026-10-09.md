## 1. Quadrivalent HPV Vaccination Before Pregnancy and Adverse Pregnancy Outcomes — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: BMJ
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-07
- **Authors**: Zhang Z et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate association between quadrivalent HPV vaccination before pregnancy and risk of adverse pregnancy outcomes.
- **Method**: Nationwide population-based matched case-control; 624,713 Swedish singleton births 2006–2023; conditional logistic regression.
- **Key findings**: HPV vaccination associated with lower preterm birth risk (aOR 0.95, 95% CI 0.91–0.99); benefit more pronounced at younger vaccination age.
- **Strengths**: National coverage, 18-year follow-up, large N, robust confounder adjustment.
- **Limitations**: Observational; residual confounding by baseline HPV infection history not fully addressable.
- **Conclusion**: Quadrivalent HPV vaccination extends benefits beyond cancer prevention to improved pregnancy outcomes.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1136/bmj-2026-100739 / PMID:42843853

## 2. Reallocation of 24-Hour Movement Behaviour Composition and Mortality: Sleep Effect Modification — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Am J Epidemiol
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-07
- **Authors**: Bian W et al.
- **Aims**: Examine mortality associations of time reallocations across device-measured 24-hour behaviours and whether sleep characteristics modify them.
- **Method**: UK Biobank prospective cohort (n=68,995); device-measured activity; compositional Cox regression; 8-year follow-up.
- **Key findings**: Replacing sleep with MVPA reduced all-cause mortality by 20% in normal sleepers (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.74–0.87); reallocation from sleep to sedentary behaviour increased CVD mortality in short sleepers.
- **Strengths**: Large population cohort, objective wearable measurement, compositional data methods handling behavioural co-dependence.
- **Limitations**: Single accelerometry wear period; UK Biobank may not represent diverse populations.
- **Conclusion**: Sleep characteristics critically modify the mortality associations of daily behavioural reallocation.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1093/aje/kwag249 / PMID:42841221

## 3. Trends in Incidence and Prevalence of Long-Term Survivors with De Novo Metastatic Incurable Cancer — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Br J Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-06
- **Authors**: Bouma R et al.
- **Aims**: Examine temporal trends in incidence and prevalence of patients surviving ≥2 years after de novo metastatic incurable solid tumour diagnosis.
- **Method**: Population-based retrospective cohort; Netherlands Cancer Registry 2000–2021; adults ≥18 years, all solid tumour types.
- **Key findings**: Annual incident cases quadrupled from 1298 (2000) to 5398 (2021); ≥18,466 patients (1 per 1000 inhabitants) alive with incurable cancer as of February 2024.
- **Strengths**: National population-based registry, 22-year temporal span, comprehensive cancer type coverage.
- **Limitations**: Dutch data; treatment details limited; registry does not capture comorbidities.
- **Conclusion**: Rapid growth in long-term incurable cancer survivors demands dedicated survivorship care research and health system planning.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03627-3 / PMID:42838997

## 4. A Preinvasive Regulatory T Cell Axis for Lung Cancer Interception — Score 6/6

- **Journal**: Nature
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-07
- **Authors**: Gamble S et al.
- **Aims**: Characterise the T cell network in preinvasive lung lesions to identify trackable biomarkers and targets for lung cancer interception.
- **Method**: Multi-omic cross-tissue immune profiling of bronchoscopy-surveilled preinvasive lesions; mouse carcinogen model with PI3Kδ inhibition intervention.
- **Key findings**: BATF+ effector Tregs accumulate in high-grade preinvasive lesions and clonally coordinated circulating eTregs track progression; PI3Kδ inhibition reduced lung tumour incidence and size.
- **Strengths**: Multi-tissue multi-omic validation in human and murine systems; identifies accessible peripheral blood biomarker.
- **Limitations**: Human cohort is bronchoscopy-selected; small preinvasive patient numbers limit statistical power.
- **Conclusion**: A conserved preinvasive eTreg circuit enables blood-based monitoring and immunological interception of early lung cancer.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-11066-6 / PMID:42844481

## 5. Circulating sCD163 as a Prognostic Macrophage Biomarker in Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Br J Cancer
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-06
- **Authors**: Hansen EB et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate clinical significance of circulating macrophage markers sCD163 and sSIRPα across prostate cancer disease stages.
- **Method**: Multi-cohort prognostic study; 646 patients (training) + 161 patients (validation); serum ELISA; multivariable Cox regression for progression-free survival.
- **Key findings**: High sCD163 independently predicted shorter PFS on first-line mCRPC treatment in both training (HR 1.77, p=0.014) and validation cohorts (HR 1.85, p=0.011).
- **Strengths**: Independent cohort validation; spans localised to metastatic disease stages; minimally invasive blood-based assay.
- **Limitations**: Single-institution cohorts; ELISA platform variability; lacks prospective interventional design.
- **Conclusion**: sCD163 is a promising minimally invasive prognostic biomarker for monitoring therapy response in mCRPC.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1038/s41416-026-03633-5 / PMID:42838995

## Appraisal notes

This week's digest is dominated by two themes: cancer prevention/surveillance epidemiology and translational biomarker science. The BMJ HPV vaccination study and Am J Epidemiol movement behaviour paper demonstrate the strength of large national registry and cohort designs for uncovering unanticipated benefits of cancer prevention interventions. The Netherlands Cancer Registry paper highlights an emerging epidemiological challenge — a rapidly growing population living long-term with incurable cancer — that will reshape survivorship research priorities. The Nature lung cancer interception paper marks a methodological advance in translational epi, combining multi-omic prospective profiling with actionable peripheral biomarker identification, bridging basic immunology and cancer screening.
