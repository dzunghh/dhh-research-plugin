## 1. Universal Germline Genetic Testing after a Diagnosis of Cancer — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Cancer Discovery
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Stadler ZK et al.
- **Aims**: Evaluate whether age at diagnosis predicts germline pathogenic variant prevalence across tumor types in a large unselected cancer cohort.
- **Method**: Pan-cancer retrospective cohort; germline sequencing of 39,184 unselected solid-malignancy patients (32 tumor types, >90 predisposition genes), stratified by SD from tumor-specific mean age.
- **Key findings**: Pathogenic variant prevalence 18.4% (early-onset), 15.6% (average-onset), 12.3% (late-onset) (p<0.001). Age-50 testing cutoff misses 4,601 carriers — 72% of all detected variants.
- **Strengths**: Largest unselected pan-cancer germline sequencing cohort to date, comprehensive gene panel irrespective of clinical suspicion.
- **Limitations**: Single-institution (MSK) limits generalizability; retrospective design.
- **Conclusion**: Hereditary risk is substantial even in late-onset cancers, supporting universal germline testing beyond conventional age criteria.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-26-0971 / PMID:42844684

## 2. Multi-institutional Patient-level Comparison of Decipher Genomic Classifier and Artera Multimodal AI in Prostate Cancer — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Clinical Cancer Research
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-08
- **Authors**: Hong BH et al.
- **Aims**: Compare concordance and prognostic value of Decipher GC and Artera MMAI across diverse multi-institutional cohorts in localized and oligometastatic prostate cancer.
- **Method**: Retrospective multi-institutional cohort (n=688); enriched for African American and East Asian patients; primary endpoint distant metastasis-free survival.
- **Key findings**: Both biomarkers were independently prognostic after adjusting for clinical variables; concordant high-risk classification associated with worst outcomes. MMAI remained significant even after adjusting for GC.
- **Strengths**: Multi-institutional design with intentional inclusion of underrepresented racial/ethnic populations; head-to-head patient-level biomarker comparison.
- **Limitations**: Retrospective design; moderate inter-tool correlation complicates discordant-case management; industry conflicts of interest present.
- **Conclusion**: Decipher GC and Artera MMAI demonstrate comparable prognostic value across diverse populations; combined classification may improve risk stratification.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-2269 / PMID:42848319
