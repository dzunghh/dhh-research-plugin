# AMSTAR 2 – appraising systematic reviews

Items are paraphrased. Source: Shea BJ et al. AMSTAR 2: a critical appraisal tool for systematic reviews that include randomised or non-randomised studies of healthcare interventions, or both. BMJ 2017;358:j4008. Full guidance: https://amstar.ca

Answer Yes / Partial Yes / No (Partial Yes exists for items 2, 4, 7, 8, 9; item 9 also has "includes only NRSI / only RCTs"; items 11, 12, 15 can be "no meta-analysis conducted"). **Critical domains are marked ★.**

| # | Item | Yes needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question and inclusion criteria include PICO components | Population, intervention, comparator, outcome stated |
| 2 ★ | Protocol established before the review; deviations justified | Partial Yes: written protocol or guide covering review question, search strategy, inclusion/exclusion criteria and RoB assessment. Yes: also registered, with a synthesis plan, a plan for investigating heterogeneity, and deviations justified |
| 3 | Choice of included study designs explained | A reason for RCT-only, NRSI-only or both |
| 4 ★ | Comprehensive search | Partial Yes: ≥ 2 databases, keywords or strategy given, restrictions justified. Yes adds reference lists, trial registries, experts, grey literature where relevant, search within 24 months |
| 5 | Study selection in duplicate | Two independent reviewers, or a sample with good agreement (at least 80%; the guidance document recommends κ ≥ 0.80) and the remainder by one reviewer |
| 6 | Data extraction in duplicate | As item 5 |
| 7 ★ | List of excluded studies with reasons | Partial Yes: list of studies excluded at full text. Yes: each exclusion also justified |
| 8 | Included studies described in adequate detail | Partial Yes: populations, interventions, comparators, outcomes and designs described. Yes: in detail, including doses, setting and follow-up |
| 9 ★ | Satisfactory risk-of-bias technique | RCTs – Partial Yes: allocation concealment, blinding; Yes adds sequence generation, selective reporting. NRSI – Partial Yes: confounding, selection bias; Yes adds exposure and outcome ascertainment, selective reporting |
| 10 | Funding sources of included studies reported | Stated for each study, or stated as not reported |
| 11 ★ | Appropriate meta-analysis methods | Justified pooling, appropriate weighting, heterogeneity investigated; NRSI pooled on confounder-adjusted estimates and kept apart from RCTs |
| 12 | Impact of risk of bias on the pooled result assessed | Only low-RoB studies included, or sensitivity/subgroup analysis by RoB |
| 13 ★ | Risk of bias accounted for when interpreting results | Discussed in the interpretation |
| 14 | Heterogeneity explained and discussed | No significant heterogeneity, or its sources investigated and impact discussed |
| 15 ★ | Publication bias investigated and its impact discussed | Graphical or statistical test, and discussion |
| 16 | Review authors' conflicts of interest and funding reported | Declared, with how any conflict was managed |

## Overall confidence in the review's results

Do not sum the items. Rate by weaknesses:

| Rating | Rule |
|---|---|
| High | No weakness, or one non-critical weakness |
| Moderate | More than one non-critical weakness, no critical flaw |
| Low | One critical flaw, with or without non-critical weaknesses |
| Critically low | More than one critical flaw |

Several non-critical weaknesses can justify moving Moderate down to Low. Appraisers may redefine the critical domains for a given question, but must do so before appraising and say so.

## Watch for
- "PRISMA-compliant" is a reporting claim; it does not answer items 2, 7, 9 or 13.
- A review of observational studies that pools crude estimates fails item 11, unless the authors justify raw data because adjusted estimates were unavailable.
- AMSTAR 2 is built for systematic reviews of healthcare interventions. Do not apply it to scoping reviews; for prevalence, diagnostic accuracy, prognostic or qualitative reviews use the JBI systematic review checklist and say AMSTAR 2 was not designed for them.
