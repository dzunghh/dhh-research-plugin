## 1. A Constrained Maximum Likelihood Method for Pleiotropy-Robust Mendelian Randomization Using Family-Based GWAS Summary Statistics — Score 5/6

- **Journal**: Statistics in Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-11
- **Authors**: Mai JH et al.
- **Type**: new method
- **Aims**: Develop MRcML-Family, an MR estimator tailored to family-based GWAS summary statistics that corrects horizontal pleiotropy while recovering power.
- **Methodology**:
  - **Design & data**: Simulations plus UK Biobank family-based GWAS summary statistics (height → BMI illustration); sample size not reported.
  - **Estimand / target**: Causal effect of exposure on outcome using direct (offspring) and indirect (parental) genetic effects.
  - **Identification**: IV relevance and exclusion; correction for horizontal pleiotropy via constraints; family-level confounding addressed; robust whether or not InSIDE holds.
  - **Estimator / model**: Constrained maximum likelihood (MRcML) jointly using offspring direct and parental indirect effects as instruments.
  - **Sensitivity / validation**: Simulations with few SNPs and small FGWAS samples; type I error, bias; UK Biobank height-BMI benchmark.
- **Software**: not reported
- **Key findings**: Near-unbiased estimates and controlled type I error in simulations; higher power than comparators; recovered the height-BMI relationship.
- **Strengths**: Addresses family-level confounding and power loss of within-family MR together, with pleiotropy robustness.
- **Limitations**: Needs FGWAS summary statistics, which are scarce and small; real-data validation is limited to one benchmark.
- **Takeaway for practice**: Consider when family-based GWAS summary data exist and population stratification or dynastic effects threaten standard MR.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1002/sim.70810 / PMID:43000927

## 3. Bayesian Hierarchical Piecewise-Exponential Cause-Specific Hazards for Posterior Predictive Validation of Competing-Risks Models — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Statistics in Medicine
- **Pub date**: 2026-11
- **Authors**: Chen H et al.
- **Type**: new method
- **Aims**: Provide a stable Bayesian competing-risks model supporting cross-validated posterior predictive log-scores, including for rare causes.
- **Methodology**:
  - **Design & data**: Simulation studies varying signal, sample size, censoring, cause imbalance; SEER colorectal cancer application.
  - **Estimand / target**: Cause-specific hazards and predictive support for event time and type.
  - **Identification**: Prediction-focused; no causal identification claimed.
  - **Estimator / model**: Piecewise-exponential cause-specific hazards with hierarchical priors on cause-specific coefficients (partial pooling).
  - **Sensitivity / validation**: Cross-validated posterior predictive log-scores; heterogeneity and imbalance scenarios.
- **Software**: not reported
- **Key findings**: Better out-of-sample predictive support and model selection; pooling narrows credible intervals for rare causes, but benefit fades under high heterogeneity.
- **Strengths**: Handles rare-cause instability with transparent bias-variance trade-off.
- **Limitations**: Prediction-oriented; piecewise-exponential baseline assumptions; no code reported.
- **Takeaway for practice**: Useful for validating competing-risks prediction models with imbalanced causes of death.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1002/sim.70812 / PMID:43001500
