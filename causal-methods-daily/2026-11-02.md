## 3. Nonparametric maximum likelihood estimation for competing risks frailty models — Score 4/6

- **Journal**: Lifetime Data Anal
- **Pub date**: 2026-10-31
- **Authors**: Chee C-S et al.
- **Type**: new method
- **Aims**: Fit shared bivariate frailty models for clustered competing risks without assuming a frailty distribution.
- **Methodology**:
  - **Design & data**: Simulations plus multi-center bladder cancer and bone marrow transplantation datasets.
  - **Estimand / target**: Cause-specific hazard regression coefficients with cluster-level frailty.
  - **Identification**: Not causal; correct specification of cause-specific frailty model, conditional independence given frailty.
  - **Estimator / model**: Nonparametric maximum likelihood (hybrid algorithm); maximum hierarchical likelihood under log-normal frailty, robustness studied theoretically.
  - **Sensitivity / validation**: Simulations under varied frailty distributions; relative bias and RMSE.
- **Software**: not reported
- **Key findings**: NPMLEs perform well overall with fast convergence; MHLEs comparable in bias/RMSE despite misspecified frailty.
- **Strengths**: Theory and simulations show log-normal MHLE robustness while offering distribution-free alternative.
- **Limitations**: Two-cause setting; no code reported.
- **Takeaway for practice**: Log-normal hierarchical-likelihood frailty fits are reasonably robust for multicenter competing risks; NPMLE is a check.
- **ID**: DOI:10.1007/s10985-026-09737-7 / PMID:42966835
